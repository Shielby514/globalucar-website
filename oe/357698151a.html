Can I keep driving with a worn brake pads?

For a short distance at reduced load, usually yes — but the risk compounds. A degraded brake pads transfers stress to neighbouring components, so a small repair can grow into a full brake system rebuild. Book the replacement once the symptom is repeatable.

Where is this brake pads located on a Audi?

Position depends on the platform. On most Audi applications the brake pads sits on the brake system assembly and can be reached from the engine bay or the underbody without removing the dashboard. Torque figures and disassembly order are given in the workshop manual for each model.

What is the bulk price for this brake pads?

Bulk pricing is tiered: 10 pieces, 50 pieces and full-carton quantities each carry a different unit rate. Piece price also moves with packing format and whether your own brand is printed on the carton, so tell us the landed quantity you are planning.

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket brake pads?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a brake pads the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.
