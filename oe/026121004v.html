How long does a water pump last on a Audi?

Treat it as a wear item: inspect every 20,000-30,000 km and replace at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance loss. Real service life swings widely with climate, road surface and duty cycle, so mileage on its own is a weak indicator.

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket water pump?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a water pump the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.
