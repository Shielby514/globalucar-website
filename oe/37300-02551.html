What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket alternator?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a alternator the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.

Who manufactures aftermarket alternator for Hyundai / Kia?

Aftermarket alternator comes from tooling houses that hold the original drawings or licensed equivalents. KingMay sources from vetted factories and inspects each batch; the manufacturer name is available on request where your market requires origin documentation.

Can I keep driving with a worn alternator?

For a short distance at reduced load, usually yes — but the risk compounds. A degraded alternator transfers stress to neighbouring components, so a small repair can grow into a full electrical & sensors rebuild. Book the replacement once the symptom is repeatable.

Why does alternator pricing vary between suppliers?

Four variables explain most of the spread: material grade, whether the goods are new production or clearance stock, packing format, and whether the quote is ex-works or landed. Ask for the material certificate alongside the price — that is what makes a comparison like-for-like.

Can KingMay supply samples of OE 37300-02551?

Yes. Sample lots of 37300-02551 are used by QC teams to benchmark dimension, material and finish before a volume commitment. Send the reference and we will quote the sample alongside the production price.

Where is this alternator located on a Hyundai / Kia?

Position depends on the platform. On most Hyundai / Kia applications the alternator sits on the electrical & sensors assembly and can be reached from the engine bay or the underbody without removing the dashboard. Torque figures and disassembly order are given in the workshop manual for each model.
