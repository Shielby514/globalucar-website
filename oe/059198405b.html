How do I look up an OE number for a Audi oil filter?

Start from the code cast or printed on the part you are removing — that is the authoritative source. If it is illegible, use the Applications table on the closest engine & fuel system reference and narrow by build year. Send photos and we will cross-check the number for you.

Can I swap OE 059198405B with another reference?

Yes, if the alternate shares the same engineering envelope. Shortlist candidates from the Related OE References table, then check bolt pattern, electrical pinout and material grade. A difference in any of those three means it is a different part, not a substitute.

Where is this oil filter located on a Audi?

Position depends on the platform. On most Audi applications the oil filter sits on the engine & fuel system assembly and can be reached from the engine bay or the underbody without removing the dashboard. Torque figures and disassembly order are given in the workshop manual for each model.

Why does oil filter pricing vary between suppliers?

Four variables explain most of the spread: material grade, whether the goods are new production or clearance stock, packing format, and whether the quote is ex-works or landed. Ask for the material certificate alongside the price — that is what makes a comparison like-for-like.

Can I keep driving with a worn oil filter?

For a short distance at reduced load, usually yes — but the risk compounds. A degraded oil filter transfers stress to neighbouring components, so a small repair can grow into a full engine & fuel system rebuild. Book the replacement once the symptom is repeatable.

How is OE 059198405B quality tested before shipment?

Incoming batches pass a dimensional check, a material look-up against the engine & fuel system specification, and a visual inspection for finish defects. Critical dimensions are measured per carton and the photographs are archived against the order number.
