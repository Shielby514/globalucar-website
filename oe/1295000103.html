What is the supersession for OE 1295000103?

A supersession happens when the maker revises a radiator and issues a new tail number. The revision normally changes a small physical detail rather than the fit. Where we know a supersession chain for 1295000103 we list the current reference; ask our desk for written confirmation before a large order.

Why does radiator pricing vary between suppliers?

Four variables explain most of the spread: material grade, whether the goods are new production or clearance stock, packing format, and whether the quote is ex-works or landed. Ask for the material certificate alongside the price — that is what makes a comparison like-for-like.

Can KingMay supply samples of OE 1295000103?

Can I keep driving with a worn radiator?

For a short distance at reduced load, usually yes — but the risk compounds. A degraded radiator transfers stress to neighbouring components, so a small repair can grow into a full cooling system rebuild. Book the replacement once the symptom is repeatable.

How do I look up an OE number for a Mercedes-Benz radiator?

Start from the code cast or printed on the part you are removing — that is the authoritative source. If it is illegible, use the Applications table on the closest cooling system reference and narrow by build year. Send photos and we will cross-check the number for you.
