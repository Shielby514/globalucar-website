Frequently Asked Questions

Is OE 032121111M compatible with my Hyundai / Kia model year?

Cross-check the OE code printed on your existing part against 032121111M. If the last 4 characters match and the vehicle falls inside the year range in the fitment table, the part is a confirmed match. KingMay's export desk also accepts VIN lookups for ambiguous cases.

What is the service interval for a thermostat housing in a Hyundai / Kia?

Most Hyundai / Kia service schedules treat the thermostat housing as a wear item with inspection every 20,000-30,000 km. Replace it at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance drop rather than on a fixed mileage, because duty cycle varies by climate and driving style.
