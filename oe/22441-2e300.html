What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket rocker arm cover sealing pad?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a rocker arm cover sealing pad the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.

What are the signs of a failing rocker arm cover sealing pad?
