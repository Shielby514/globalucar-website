Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the radiator fan located on a Mercedes-Benz vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Mercedes-Benz applications this radiator fan is fitted on the cooling system assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What are the symptoms of a worn-out radiator fan?

Common failure signs for this radiator fan include noise on start-up, vibration under load, warning lights on the cluster, fluid leaks or uneven operation. Once any of these appears the OE reference 0999061100 should be inspected and replaced if out of spec.
