Why is my Mercedes-Benz making noise from the radiator?

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket radiator?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a radiator the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.
