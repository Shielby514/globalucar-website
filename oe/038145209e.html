Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the vacuum pump, brake system located on a Audi vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Audi applications this vacuum pump, brake system is fitted on the brake system assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What is the service interval for a vacuum pump, brake system in a Audi?

Most Audi service schedules treat the vacuum pump, brake system as a wear item with inspection every 20,000-30,000 km. Replace it at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance drop rather than on a fixed mileage, because duty cycle varies by climate and driving style.
