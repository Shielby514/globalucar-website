Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the arm complete located on a Hyundai / Kia vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Hyundai / Kia applications this arm complete is fitted on the suspension, steering & brake assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What are the symptoms of a worn-out arm complete?

Common failure signs for this arm complete include noise on start-up, vibration under load, warning lights on the cluster, fluid leaks or uneven operation. Once any of these appears the OE reference 54501-25000 should be inspected and replaced if out of spec.

Can I replace OE 54501-25000 with a different reference?

Yes, provided the alternate reference shares the same engineering envelope. Use the Related OE References table on this page to identify candidates, then confirm bolt pattern, electrical pinout and material grade before placing a bulk order.
