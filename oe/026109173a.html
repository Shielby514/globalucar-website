Can I keep driving with a worn timing system cover?

For a short distance at reduced load, usually yes — but the risk compounds. A degraded timing system cover transfers stress to neighbouring components, so a small repair can grow into a full engine & fuel system rebuild. Book the replacement once the symptom is repeatable.

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket timing system cover?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a timing system cover the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.
