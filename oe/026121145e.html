What is the service interval for a coolant flange in a Audi?

Most Audi service schedules treat the coolant flange as a wear item with inspection every 20,000-30,000 km. Replace it at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance drop rather than on a fixed mileage, because duty cycle varies by climate and driving style.
