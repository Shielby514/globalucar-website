Why does piston pin snap pricing vary between suppliers?

Four variables explain most of the spread: material grade, whether the goods are new production or clearance stock, packing format, and whether the quote is ex-works or landed. Ask for the material certificate alongside the price — that is what makes a comparison like-for-like.

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket piston pin snap?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a piston pin snap the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.
