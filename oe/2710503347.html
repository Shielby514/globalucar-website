What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket camshaft adjuster?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a camshaft adjuster the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.

Can I keep driving with a worn camshaft adjuster?

For a short distance at reduced load, usually yes — but the risk compounds. A degraded camshaft adjuster transfers stress to neighbouring components, so a small repair can grow into a full engine & fuel system rebuild. Book the replacement once the symptom is repeatable.
