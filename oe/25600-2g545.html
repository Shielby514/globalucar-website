What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket cooling water temperature control assembly?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a cooling water temperature control assembly the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.
