Bulk pricing is tiered: 10 pieces, 50 pieces and full-carton quantities each carry a different unit rate. Piece price also moves with packing format and whether your own brand is printed on the carton, so tell us the landed quantity you are planning.

Where is this coolant pipe located on a Volkswagen?

Position depends on the platform. On most Volkswagen applications the coolant pipe sits on the cooling system assembly and can be reached from the engine bay or the underbody without removing the dashboard. Torque figures and disassembly order are given in the workshop manual for each model.

What if OE 028121065K arrives defective?

Report it with the order number and packing-list line plus photographs. Because every carton is photographed before loading, we can trace the batch immediately and settle the claim against the inspection record instead of arguing from memory.

Who manufactures aftermarket coolant pipe for Volkswagen?

Aftermarket coolant pipe comes from tooling houses that hold the original drawings or licensed equivalents. KingMay sources from vetted factories and inspects each batch; the manufacturer name is available on request where your market requires origin documentation.

How long does a coolant pipe last on a Volkswagen?

Treat it as a wear item: inspect every 20,000-30,000 km and replace at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance loss. Real service life swings widely with climate, road surface and duty cycle, so mileage on its own is a weak indicator.

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket coolant pipe?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a coolant pipe the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.
