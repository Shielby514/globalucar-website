What are the symptoms of a worn-out inner ball joint assembly?

Common failure signs for this inner ball joint assembly include noise on start-up, vibration under load, warning lights on the cluster, fluid leaks or uneven operation. Once any of these appears the OE reference 56540-C9000 should be inspected and replaced if out of spec.
