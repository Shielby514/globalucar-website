What is the supersession for OE 070199231A?

A supersession happens when the maker revises a engine mount and issues a new tail number. The revision normally changes a small physical detail rather than the fit. Where we know a supersession chain for 070199231A we list the current reference; ask our desk for written confirmation before a large order.

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket engine mount?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a engine mount the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.
