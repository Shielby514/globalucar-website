Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the wheel hub bearing located on a Audi vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Audi applications this wheel hub bearing is fitted on the suspension, steering & brake assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What is the service interval for a wheel hub bearing in a Audi?

Most Audi service schedules treat the wheel hub bearing as a wear item with inspection every 20,000-30,000 km. Replace it at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance drop rather than on a fixed mileage, because duty cycle varies by climate and driving style.
