Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the front brake disc located on a Mercedes-Benz vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Mercedes-Benz applications this front brake disc is fitted on the brake system assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What are the symptoms of a worn-out front brake disc?

Common failure signs for this front brake disc include noise on start-up, vibration under load, warning lights on the cluster, fluid leaks or uneven operation. Once any of these appears the OE reference 0155212033 should be inspected and replaced if out of spec.
