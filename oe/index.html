How this library is built

References are compiled from manufacturer parts catalogues, dealer parts listings and cross-reference tables, then normalised to the hyphen-free form used across our catalog. Component family decoding follows the Hyundai-Kia 5+5 numbering convention (five-digit family prefix + five-character variant suffix). Verified applications are published in the fitment table of each page; anything advisory is labelled as such. Numbers we cannot confirm are never published — that is why the library grows in small, steady batches rather than all at once.
