Can I swap OE 0012957606 with another reference?

Yes, if the alternate shares the same engineering envelope. Shortlist candidates from the Related OE References table, then check bolt pattern, electrical pinout and material grade. A difference in any of those three means it is a different part, not a substitute.

Why does clutch master cylinder pricing vary between suppliers?

Can I keep driving with a worn clutch master cylinder?

For a short distance at reduced load, usually yes — but the risk compounds. A degraded clutch master cylinder transfers stress to neighbouring components, so a small repair can grow into a full brake system rebuild. Book the replacement once the symptom is repeatable.
