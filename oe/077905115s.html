How do I verify OE 077905115S is the correct number?

Why is my Volkswagen making noise from the ignition coil?

Noise normally traces to worn internal surfaces, a loose mount, a dry bearing or contact with a neighbouring part. Isolate it by reproducing the sound at a fixed speed, then compare against the reference photo for 077905115S to confirm the right replacement.

Which model years use OE 077905115S?

Build-year coverage is listed row by row in the Applications table above. Where several generations share one number, the newer rows usually carry a revised suffix — compare the last four characters before buying, since a suffix change often means a different connector or bracket.

What is the supersession for OE 077905115S?

A supersession happens when the maker revises a ignition coil and issues a new tail number. The revision normally changes a small physical detail rather than the fit. Where we know a supersession chain for 077905115S we list the current reference; ask our desk for written confirmation before a large order.

How do I look up an OE number for a Volkswagen ignition coil?

Start from the code cast or printed on the part you are removing — that is the authoritative source. If it is illegible, use the Applications table on the closest electrical & sensors reference and narrow by build year. Send photos and we will cross-check the number for you.
