What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket brake pads?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a brake pads the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.

How is OE 0024205220 quality tested before shipment?

Incoming batches pass a dimensional check, a material look-up against the brake system specification, and a visual inspection for finish defects. Critical dimensions are measured per carton and the photographs are archived against the order number.

Can I swap OE 0024205220 with another reference?

Yes, if the alternate shares the same engineering envelope. Shortlist candidates from the Related OE References table, then check bolt pattern, electrical pinout and material grade. A difference in any of those three means it is a different part, not a substitute.
