Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the front shock absorber located on a Mercedes-Benz vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Mercedes-Benz applications this front shock absorber is fitted on the suspension, steering & brake assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What is the service interval for a front shock absorber in a Mercedes-Benz?

Most Mercedes-Benz service schedules treat the front shock absorber as a wear item with inspection every 20,000-30,000 km. Replace it at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance drop rather than on a fixed mileage, because duty cycle varies by climate and driving style.
