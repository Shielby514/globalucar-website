Frequently Asked Questions

What vehicles does OE 0004200720 fit?

OE 0004200720 is a factory reference for brake pads on selected Mercedes-Benz models. The full fitment list is published in the Applications table above — verify trim level and build year against your local fleet before placing a wholesale order.

What is the service interval for a brake pads in a Mercedes-Benz?

Most Mercedes-Benz service schedules treat the brake pads as a wear item with inspection every 20,000-30,000 km. Replace it at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance drop rather than on a fixed mileage, because duty cycle varies by climate and driving style.

Can I replace OE 0004200720 with a different reference?
