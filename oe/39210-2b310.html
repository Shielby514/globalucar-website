Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the oxygen sensor located on a Hyundai / Kia vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Hyundai / Kia applications this oxygen sensor is fitted on the engine & fuel system assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What are the symptoms of a worn-out oxygen sensor?

Common failure signs for this oxygen sensor include noise on start-up, vibration under load, warning lights on the cluster, fluid leaks or uneven operation. Once any of these appears the OE reference 39210-2B310 should be inspected and replaced if out of spec.
