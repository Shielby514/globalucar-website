What is the supersession for OE 54530-4A001?

A supersession happens when the maker revises a ball joint and issues a new tail number. The revision normally changes a small physical detail rather than the fit. Where we know a supersession chain for 54530-4A001 we list the current reference; ask our desk for written confirmation before a large order.

Where is this ball joint located on a Hyundai / Kia?

Position depends on the platform. On most Hyundai / Kia applications the ball joint sits on the suspension, steering & brake assembly and can be reached from the engine bay or the underbody without removing the dashboard. Torque figures and disassembly order are given in the workshop manual for each model.

How long does a ball joint last on a Hyundai / Kia?

Treat it as a wear item: inspect every 20,000-30,000 km and replace at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance loss. Real service life swings widely with climate, road surface and duty cycle, so mileage on its own is a weak indicator.
