What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket complete parts of the front and lower arms?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a complete parts of the front and lower arms the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.

What is the lead time for OE 54500-C1000?

Stock references leave Guangzhou within 7 working days. If 54500-C1000 needs a production run, allow 25-35 days depending on quantity and surface treatment. The schedule is confirmed in writing when the order is placed.

Why does complete parts of the front and lower arms pricing vary between suppliers?

Four variables explain most of the spread: material grade, whether the goods are new production or clearance stock, packing format, and whether the quote is ex-works or landed. Ask for the material certificate alongside the price — that is what makes a comparison like-for-like.

Why is my Hyundai / Kia making noise from the complete parts of the front and lower arms?

Noise normally traces to worn internal surfaces, a loose mount, a dry bearing or contact with a neighbouring part. Isolate it by reproducing the sound at a fixed speed, then compare against the reference photo for 54500-C1000 to confirm the right replacement.

How do I look up an OE number for a Hyundai / Kia complete parts of the front and lower arms?

Start from the code cast or printed on the part you are removing — that is the authoritative source. If it is illegible, use the Applications table on the closest suspension, steering & brake reference and narrow by build year. Send photos and we will cross-check the number for you.
