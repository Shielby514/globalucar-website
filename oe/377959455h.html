How long does a radiator fan last on a Volkswagen?

Treat it as a wear item: inspect every 20,000-30,000 km and replace at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance loss. Real service life swings widely with climate, road surface and duty cycle, so mileage on its own is a weak indicator.

Where is this radiator fan located on a Volkswagen?

Position depends on the platform. On most Volkswagen applications the radiator fan sits on the cooling system assembly and can be reached from the engine bay or the underbody without removing the dashboard. Torque figures and disassembly order are given in the workshop manual for each model.
