Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the bush located on a Hyundai / Kia vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Hyundai / Kia applications this bush is fitted on the suspension, steering & brake assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What are the symptoms of a worn-out bush?

Common failure signs for this bush include noise on start-up, vibration under load, warning lights on the cluster, fluid leaks or uneven operation. Once any of these appears the OE reference 55257-2S000 should be inspected and replaced if out of spec.
