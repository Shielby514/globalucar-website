What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket clutch master cylinder?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a clutch master cylinder the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.

What are the signs of a failing clutch master cylinder?

Watch for noise on cold start, vibration under load, warning lights, fluid seepage or uneven operation. Once any of those appears the clutch master cylinder should be inspected, and reference 251721401F replaced if it measures outside specification.
