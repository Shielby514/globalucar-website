Where is this carburetor flange located on a Audi?

Position depends on the platform. On most Audi applications the carburetor flange sits on the engine & fuel system assembly and can be reached from the engine bay or the underbody without removing the dashboard. Torque figures and disassembly order are given in the workshop manual for each model.

Can I swap OE 026129761A with another reference?

How do I verify OE 026129761A is the correct number?

Three checks: the code printed on your old part must read 026129761A; the number must fall inside the build-year range in the Applications table; and the connector, thread or bolt pattern must match the reference photo. Codes cast into the housing are the most reliable source.

What is the bulk price for this carburetor flange?

Bulk pricing is tiered: 10 pieces, 50 pieces and full-carton quantities each carry a different unit rate. Piece price also moves with packing format and whether your own brand is printed on the carton, so tell us the landed quantity you are planning.

How long does a carburetor flange last on a Audi?

Treat it as a wear item: inspect every 20,000-30,000 km and replace at the first sign of leakage, noise or performance loss. Real service life swings widely with climate, road surface and duty cycle, so mileage on its own is a weak indicator.
