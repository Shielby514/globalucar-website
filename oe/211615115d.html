Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the brake pads located on a Nissan vehicle?

Mounting position depends on the platform. For most Nissan applications this brake pads is fitted on the brake system assembly and is reachable from the engine bay or underbody without dashboard removal. Workshop manuals list torque specs and disassembly sequence per model.

What are the symptoms of a worn-out brake pads?

Common failure signs for this brake pads include noise on start-up, vibration under load, warning lights on the cluster, fluid leaks or uneven operation. Once any of these appears the OE reference 211615115D should be inspected and replaced if out of spec.
