What is the supersession for OE 35320-2G740?

A supersession happens when the maker revises a high pressure fuel pump and issues a new tail number. The revision normally changes a small physical detail rather than the fit. Where we know a supersession chain for 35320-2G740 we list the current reference; ask our desk for written confirmation before a large order.

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket high pressure fuel pump?

OEM means the part was produced for the vehicle maker's assembly line; aftermarket means it is made independently to the same engineering drawing. For a high pressure fuel pump the practical gap is usually tooling age and packaging rather than fit, provided the tolerance class matches.

Where is this high pressure fuel pump located on a Hyundai / Kia?

Position depends on the platform. On most Hyundai / Kia applications the high pressure fuel pump sits on the engine & fuel system assembly and can be reached from the engine bay or the underbody without removing the dashboard. Torque figures and disassembly order are given in the workshop manual for each model.
