Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Guangzhou Vehicle External Trade Co., Ltd (“Globalucar”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit www.global-ucars.com or contact us through our website, email, WhatsApp or third-party services.

1. Who We Are

Globalucar is a licensed vehicle and auto-parts exporter based in Guangzhou, China, serving B2B buyers worldwide. For any privacy-related request, contact us at ucars@global-ucars.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following information when you submit an inquiry, request a quotation or communicate with us:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number, country and company name;
  • Inquiry content: products of interest, quantities, messages and attachments you send us;
  • Technical data collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and referring URLs (via server logs and Google Analytics).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to inquiries and provide quotations and after-sales support;
  • To process orders, arrange shipping and complete export documentation;
  • To improve our website, product catalogue and customer service;
  • To comply with applicable export-control, tax and legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site and to improve content. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will remain functional. Data collected via Google Analytics is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

5. Data Sharing

We share personal data only with: (a) logistics and freight partners, to the extent necessary to fulfil your orders; (b) payment providers, for transaction processing; (c) authorities, where required by law. All sharing is limited to the minimum necessary.

6. Data Retention & Security

Inquiry and order records are retained only as long as needed for business and legal purposes. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing ucars@global-ucars.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites (e.g. social media, marketplaces). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their policies separately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with the revision date shown at the top.
