About Our Suspension & Chassis Parts Catalog

Suspension, steering and brake parts represent the largest replacement volume category in the global aftermarket by unit count. These safety-critical components require OEM-grade manufacturing tolerances, making supplier selection and quality documentation critical for importers and distributors.

Our suspension catalog covers: steering & linkage (tie rod ends, rack ends, idler arms, pitman arms), control & geometry (control arms, upper/lower ball joints, stabilizer bar links), damping (shock absorbers, strut assemblies, spring isolators), wheel & bearing (wheel bearing hub assemblies, CV axles, drive shafts) and brakes (brake calipers, rotors/discs, brake pads, drum brake assemblies).
