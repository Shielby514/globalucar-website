How to Register for Canton Fair as an Overseas Buyer: Badge, Invitation Letter & Visa

📅 2026-10-09 ⏱ 12 min read ✍️ Globalucar Editorial

Short answer: Overseas buyers register free through the official Canton Fair Buyer Service System, upload passport and company details, apply for the overseas buyer badge, and download the invitation letter from the same portal. The badge gets you into the Pazhou halls; the invitation letter supports a China business (M) visa if your passport needs one. Nationals from about 50 countries can enter visa-free for 30 days until 31 December 2026, but they still need the buyer badge.

1. Who Needs to Register for Canton Fair?

Every overseas visitor who wants to walk the exhibition halls during the 140th Canton Fair needs a buyer badge. The rule applies to first-time buyers and returning buyers alike. Pre-registration is now mandatory for all offline attendees, according to the Ministry of Commerce announcement for the spring session. Once approved, the badge is usually valid for the entire session and can remain valid for future fairs, so returning buyers should keep the physical card safe.

Registration is free when done through the official buyer portal. Third-party agents who charge for a "VIP badge" or "fast-track invitation" are selling something the fair itself gives away. The only official route is the Buyer Service System, and the only costs you should expect are the visa fee at your local Chinese consulate and the 100 RMB on-site service fee if you decide to register at the venue instead of pre-registering.

2. What the Buyer Badge Actually Grants You

The badge is more than entry paper. It carries your name, nationality, company and a QR code that exhibitors scan when they want to record your details. That scan becomes the first record in your post-fair follow-up. Without it, you cannot enter the Canton Fair Complex during Phase 1 (October 15–19), Phase 2 (October 23–27) or Phase 3 (October 31 – November 4).

Beyond hall entry, pre-registered buyers receive fair updates by email, can book badge collection slots at airport and hotel counters, and are eligible for small souvenirs at the registration desks. The practical value is time: pre-registered buyers skip the longest lines at the Pazhou complex on opening morning.

3. Step-by-Step: Canton Fair Buyer Registration

The current registration flow runs through the Canton Fair Buyer Service System. The interface has been consolidated there, so if you see an older invitation-only portal that shows limited functions, switch to the main buyer address. The system asks for the same documents whether you are a garage owner, auto parts distributor or fleet buyer.

  1. Create a buyer account. Use a working email and set a password. The account stays active for future fairs.
  2. Fill in personal details. Upload a passport copy and a passport-style photo. The photo must have a plain background; casual photos are often rejected.
  3. Add company information. Enter your registered business name, address and trade role. This name should match your business card and visa application.
  4. Select attendance intent. Choose the 140th session, your product interests and the phases you plan to attend.
  5. Apply for the overseas buyer badge. Tick the option to generate the invitation letter at the same time.
  6. Download the invitation letter. Once approved, print it for your visa appointment and keep a PDF on your phone.

Processing times vary, but most buyers receive badge approval within a few business days. Apply at least three weeks before travel to leave time for visa processing and flight booking.

4. Invitation Letter vs. Visa: Do You Need Both?

The invitation letter and the visa are two separate things. The invitation letter proves to the Chinese consulate that you have a commercial reason to visit. The visa is the sticker or stamp that lets you enter China. Whether you need a visa depends on your passport, not on the invitation letter.

About 50 nationalities currently enjoy 30-day visa-free entry to China for business or tourism until the end of 2026. Buyers from those countries do not need an M visa, but they still need the Canton Fair buyer badge to enter the halls. The invitation letter can still be useful as supporting documentation at immigration, but it is not mandatory for visa-free travelers.

5. China Business Visa (M Visa) Checklist

If your passport requires a visa, the M visa is the standard category for commercial activity, including trade fairs. The Canton Fair invitation letter satisfies the invitation-document requirement. Your local Chinese embassy or Visa Application Service Centre decides the final validity, number of entries and stay length.

The most common mistake is mismatching passport numbers. If you apply for the invitation letter in August and renew your passport in September, the numbers will not match the visa application. Re-download the invitation letter after any passport change. Another common error is applying at the wrong mission: the country where you legally reside, not the country of your passport, usually handles the application.

6. Where to Collect Your Badge in Guangzhou

Pre-registered buyers can collect the badge before reaching the Pazhou complex. Collection points for the 140th session include Baiyun International Airport, Guangzhou South railway station, the Pazhou Hong Kong and Macao passenger terminal, selected hotels and the fair's Hong Kong office. On-site collection at Pazhou is also available but usually slower.

Bring your original passport and a printed copy of the invitation letter or badge confirmation. Some counters accept the QR code from the official app, but a paper backup removes any language-barrier friction. Airport counters are often the fastest because staff there are used to handling international arrivals.

7. Visa-Free Entry and the 240-Hour Transit Option

For buyers from visa-free countries, the process is simpler but not automatic. You still need a return ticket, a hotel booking and a clear reason for entry. Carrying the Canton Fair invitation letter, badge confirmation and a short list of suppliers to visit makes the immigration conversation faster.

The 240-hour visa-free transit rule lets eligible travelers pass through China for up to ten days if they are continuing to a third country or region. Hong Kong is about one hour from Guangzhou by high-speed rail, so a routing such as London–Guangzhou–Hong Kong–London can fit the transit rule. This route does not require an M visa, but you must hold confirmed onward tickets and stay within the permitted transit area. Rules can change, so verify the current list of eligible ports and nationalities before relying on this option.

8. Common Registration Mistakes to Avoid

Small errors in the registration phase can cost a buyer the first day of the fair. The registration system is automated, so mismatched data is rejected or delayed without human explanation.

  • Using a trading name instead of the registered company name. The badge name and visa documents should match your business card and company registration.
  • Uploading a low-quality photo. The system compares the image to passport standards. A dark, cropped or filtered photo usually fails.
  • Waiting until arrival to register. On-site queues are longest on October 15. Pre-register and collect the badge at the airport.
  • Ignoring passport expiry. Many countries require six months of validity beyond your planned return date. Check early.
  • Applying at the wrong visa centre. Use the mission that serves your country of legal residence, not necessarily your nationality.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for Canton Fair as an overseas buyer?

Register free on the official Canton Fair Buyer Service System. Create an account, upload your passport and a passport-style photo, enter your company details, apply for the overseas buyer badge, and generate the invitation letter from the same portal.

Is the Canton Fair invitation letter free?

Yes. The official invitation letter is free for approved overseas buyers when generated through the Buyer Service System. You do not need to pay an agency for it.

Do I need a visa if I have a Canton Fair buyer badge?

The badge gets you into the fair, but it does not replace a visa. Nationals of visa-free countries can enter without a visa until 31 December 2026; others need an M business visa supported by the invitation letter.

Can I register for Canton Fair on the day of the fair?

On-site registration is possible but not recommended. It costs about 100 RMB, requires a paper photo, and involves longer queues than pre-registration.

How long does Canton Fair buyer registration take?

Most buyers receive badge approval within a few business days. Allow at least three weeks before travel so you have time for visa processing and flight booking.

Where can I pick up my Canton Fair badge in Guangzhou?

Pre-registered buyers can collect badges at Baiyun Airport, Guangzhou South railway station, the Pazhou Hong Kong and Macao terminal, selected hotels and the fair's Hong Kong office. Bring your original passport and badge confirmation.
