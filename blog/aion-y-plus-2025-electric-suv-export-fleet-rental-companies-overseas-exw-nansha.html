Why Fleet & Rental Operators Are Choosing the AION Y Plus in 2025

The commercial EV fleet market outside China is at an inflection point. Fuel costs have doubled in most emerging markets over the past four years, and ride-hail platforms such as Grab, Careem, InDriver and Yango now offer preferential dispatch priority to zero-emission vehicles in a growing list of cities. For rental and taxi operators, the AION Y Plus has become the reference model: a purpose-engineered electric SUV from GAC Group's dedicated EV arm, priced low enough to replace internal combustion economy models yet equipped with the cabin technology that fleet drivers and passengers demand.

GAC Aion recorded cumulative deliveries of over 1 million units by 2024 — an achievement shared by fewer than a dozen EV brands globally. The Y Plus, as the volume flagship of the AION lineup, accounts for the majority of those deliveries and has established a dense service network across Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Central Asia that directly benefits overseas fleet operators purchasing new units from China.

  • Driver biometric fatigue detection: Camera-based drowsiness alert is standard on the Smart Edition — a compliance requirement for taxi and ride-hail licensing in Malaysia, Thailand and the UAE.

Key Export Markets: Southeast Asia, Middle East & Central Asia Rental Sectors

Globalucar has identified three primary regional opportunities for AION Y Plus fleet exports based on charging infrastructure maturity, EV incentive regimes and rental market demand:

Southeast Asia: Thailand, Malaysia & Indonesia

Thailand has emerged as the most advanced EV market in ASEAN. The Thai government's 30@30 policy (30% EV production by 2030) includes import duty reductions of 2–8% for CBU electric vehicles, and Bangkok's Grab and Bolt platforms now actively prioritise EV drivers with lower commission rates. GAC Aion has an existing assembly agreement in Thailand, which means local service network coverage is available for fleet operators.

Malaysia eliminated import duties on CBU EVs through end-2025 (extension under review for 2026). Kuala Lumpur's MyTeksi and Grab operations have both committed to full electrification of their leased fleet segments by 2027, creating structured demand for new Chinese EV imports with sub-$15,000 acquisition costs.

Indonesia levies a 15% import tariff on CBU EVs, but battery-electric vehicles are exempt from the 15% luxury goods tax (PPnBM) that applies to ICE vehicles in the same price segment, broadly equalising landed cost. Jakarta's BlueBird taxi group is actively evaluating Chinese BEV models for its next procurement cycle.

Middle East: UAE, Saudi Arabia & Qatar

GCC countries impose a unified 5% customs duty on vehicles under the Common External Tariff. With no VAT on vehicle purchases in the UAE (Jebel Ali Free Zone) and Qatar, the effective landed cost for an AION Y Plus Smart Edition in Dubai is approximately $14,200–$15,800 depending on freight routing. Both Careem (owned by Uber) and InDriver have launched EV-priority driver incentive schemes in Dubai and Riyadh during 2025, with fleet operators receiving rebates of AED 500–1,200 per month for fully electric vehicles.

Central Asia: Uzbekistan & Kazakhstan

Uzbekistan is the fastest-growing EV market in Central Asia, with government data showing a 340% year-on-year increase in EV registrations through Q1 2026. The country has eliminated import duties on EVs entirely through end-2026. Chinese brands — particularly BYD, Chery and GAC Aion — account for over 70% of new EV sales. Tashkent-based rental operators and the Yandex.Taxi fleet division have been the primary volume buyers. Kazakhstan similarly imposes zero customs duty on EVs and is expanding its network of Chinese-standard DC fast-chargers in Almaty and Astana.

Compliance, Homologation & Charging Infrastructure Guide

Successful fleet deployment of Chinese EVs in overseas markets requires advance planning across three compliance dimensions: vehicle type approval, charging connector standards and battery disposal regulations.

Vehicle Type Approval by Market

  • Thailand (TISI): Chinese EVs require TISI 2127-2563 certification or an exemption under the EV30@30 programme. GAC Aion has existing TISI-approved variants for the Y series; Globalucar can supply the relevant certification letter with each shipment.
  • Malaysia (SIRIM): EVs require SIRIM-approved type approval. AION Y Plus is eligible under the Malaysian Approved Permit (AP) system. Buyers must apply for an Open AP through approved importers.
  • UAE (ESMA): Vehicles must carry a valid Certificate of Conformity to UN/ECE regulations. The AION Y Plus 2025 complies with UN/ECE R100 (electric powertrain safety), R48 (lighting) and R94/R95 (collision safety). ESMA registration is typically completed in 15–20 working days.
  • Uzbekistan / Kazakhstan: EAC (Eurasian Conformity) certification required. GAC Aion holds EAC approval for the Y Plus in the Eurasian Economic Union — Globalucar can provide the relevant EAC certificates on request.

Charging Connector Compatibility

The AION Y Plus 2025 ships with GB/T AC (slow) and GB/T DC (fast) connectors as standard. For export markets, Globalucar provides the following:

  • Type 2 AC adapter (included) — for European-standard AC wallboxes used in Malaysia and UAE
  • CCS2 DC adapter (on request) — for Combo2 fast-charger networks in Thailand and Indonesia
  • CHAdeMO adapter (on request) — for legacy Japanese-standard chargers in some markets

Note: Central Asian charging networks (Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan) are rapidly deploying GB/T-standard fast chargers supplied by Chinese infrastructure companies (State Grid, TELD, StarCharge). Fleet operators in these markets can use the vehicle's native connectors without adaptation.

Q5: Does the AION Y Plus support right-hand drive (RHD) configuration?
The standard production AION Y Plus is left-hand drive (LHD) only. Q6: What is the battery warranty for fleet use?
The GAC Aion standard battery warranty is 8 years / 160,000 km for capacity retention above 70% — one of the longest in its class.
