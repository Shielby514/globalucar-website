2026 Jetta VS5 from China: VW Group Engineering for Budget-Conscious Importers — Complete Guide

📅 May 16, 2026 ⏱ 9 min read ✍️ Globalucar Editorial
Quick Answer: The 2026 Jetta VS5 from FAW-VW China offers MQB A0 platform, 1.4 TSI 150hp and 7-speed DSG at US $13,400 FOB Guangzhou — the most affordable VW Group-engineered SUV available for export. Two variants (Comfort and Premium) with full specs, target markets and ordering details.

The Jetta Brand: VW Group’s Value Play for China and the World

In 2019, Volkswagen Group spun off the Jetta nameplate into a standalone sub-brand for the Chinese market — positioned below the main Volkswagen marque in price, but engineered to the same fundamental standards. The logic was clear: millions of Chinese buyers wanted the reliability and resale value associated with VW Group engineering but could not stretch to Volkswagen-badge pricing.

The strategy worked. Jetta-branded vehicles have sold consistently since launch, with the VS5 compact SUV becoming the brand’s volume leader. For export markets, the Jetta brand creates an opportunity that few importers have fully explored: genuine VW Group engineering at prices below Skoda.

The 2026 Jetta VS5 is manufactured by the FAW-Volkswagen joint venture in Changchun, Jilin Province — one of China’s most established automotive manufacturing centres. FAW-VW is the same joint venture that produces the Audi A4L, Audi Q5L and VW Bora for the Chinese market. The VS5 rolls off the same production lines, subject to the same quality control processes.

Jetta VS5: MQB A0 Engineering at Entry-Level Pricing

The VS5 is built on the MQB A0 platform — the same architecture used by the VW Polo, VW T-Cross, Seat Ibiza and Skoda Fabia. While MQB A0 is the entry tier of the MQB family (below the full MQB used by Golf and Tiguan), it shares the same engineering philosophy: modular construction, transverse engine mounting, standardised electronics architecture and high-strength steel body construction.

Core Powertrain

  • Engine: 1.4 TSI EA211 — 110 kW (150 hp) @ 5,000–6,000 rpm, 250 Nm torque @ 1,400–3,500 rpm. This is the same engine used across dozens of VW, Audi, Skoda and Seat models globally.
  • Transmission: 7-speed DSG dual-clutch (DQ200) with paddle-shift capability on Premium variant. Aisin 6-speed automatic available as an option on the Comfort variant.
  • Performance: 0–100 km/h in 9.5 seconds, top speed 190 km/h, combined fuel consumption 6.9–7.1 L/100 km (WLTC).

Body and Dimensions

  • 5-door compact SUV body style
  • Dimensions: 4,418 × 1,841 × 1,605 mm (Comfort) / 4,418 × 1,841 × 1,616 mm (Premium)
  • Wheelbase: 2,630–2,638 mm
  • Boot volume: 390–482 L depending on variant — among the largest in the compact SUV class
  • Fuel tank: 51–55 L
  • Curb weight: 1,355 kg

Chassis and Safety

  • Front MacPherson strut / Rear multi-link suspension
  • 78% high-strength steel body construction
  • 6 airbags standard (dual front, dual side, dual curtain)
  • ESP electronic stability, ABS+EBD, traction control, hill-hold assist
  • FAW-VW MIIT export certification: chassis FV7140LAAEG, announcement MN5029XGJ11

VS5 Comfort Edition vs Premium Edition: What Changes at $1,700 More

The VS5 is available in two export variants, giving importers a good/better option within a single model line:

Comfort Edition — US $13,400 FOB

The value-oriented variant. Equipped with all essential features for daily driving and export compliance:

  • 1.4 TSI engine (150 hp) with 7-speed DSG
  • Basic infotainment with Bluetooth and USB
  • Manual air conditioning
  • Fabric seats
  • 16-inch steel wheels with hubcaps
  • Basic ADAS: AEB, LKA, rear parking sensors
  • Product ID: 113

Premium Edition — US $15,264 FOB (Est.)

The fully-equipped variant with the features that close retail sales in competitive showrooms:

  • All Comfort Edition mechanicals
  • 360° panoramic camera with bird’s-eye view
  • 10-inch touchscreen with J-Connect system, wireless CarPlay, navigation
  • Dual-zone automatic climate control
  • Perforated PVC leather seats with heating function
  • 18-inch alloy wheels
  • Keyless entry and start
  • Full ADAS suite: ACC adaptive cruise, front assist with emergency braking, BSD blind spot detection
  • Armoured front grille and “Cloud Wing” rear spoiler for a more premium appearance
  • Product ID: 114

The $1,700 upgrade from Comfort to Premium buys features that typically add $3,000–$4,000 to the retail price in destination markets. For importers focused on showroom appeal and buyer conversion, the Premium Edition is the better investment.

VS5 vs Tharu XR vs T-Roc: The VW Group Three-Tier SUV Ladder

The Jetta VS5, Skoda Tharu XR and VW T-Roc form a natural three-tier ladder within the VW Group export portfolio. Each occupies a distinct price segment while sharing fundamental MQB engineering:

Model Brand FOB Price Positioning
Jetta VS5 Jetta (FAW-VW) $13,400 Entry: maximum price competitiveness
Skoda Tharu XR Skoda (SAIC-VW) $14,500 Mid: more space and ground clearance
VW T-Roc Black Volkswagen (SAIC-VW) $19,500 Premium: VW badge, sporty styling, 2.0 TSI option

Importers can use this three-tier structure to offer customers a good/better/best choice within a single VW Group portfolio — reducing supplier complexity, standardising parts inventory and simplifying documentation.

Best Markets for the VS5

The Jetta VS5 is optimally positioned for markets where:

  • VW Group brand recognition drives buyer confidence but sticker price is a critical barrier (many African, Middle Eastern and Central Asian markets)
  • Japanese compact SUVs dominate (Toyota Yaris Cross, Honda HR-V, Nissan Kicks) and there is demand for a European alternative at comparable pricing
  • Aftermarket parts supply chain favours VW Group vehicles (many markets have established VW/Skoda parts importers)
  • Fleet operators need reliable, economical compact SUVs for taxi, ride-hailing or government fleet applications

Top target markets: Middle East (Iraq, Saudi Arabia, UAE), Africa (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya LHD), Southeast Asia (Philippines, Indonesia), Central Asia (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan), Latin America (Chile, Colombia, Peru).

For importers targeting price-sensitive markets where the VW name carries weight, the VS5 is the tool to capture that demand without overstretching buyer budgets.
