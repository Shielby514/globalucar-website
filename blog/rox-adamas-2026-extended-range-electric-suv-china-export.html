2026 ROX Adamas EREV: Full Specs, FOB Pricing & Import Guide for Global Buyers

📅 May 14, 2026 ⏱ 10 min read ✍️ Globalucar Editorial
Quick Answer: 2026 ROX Adamas EREV — 1,405 km total range, 350 kW dual-motor AWD, 7-seat luxury off-road SUV. FOB pricing from USD 51,500, full specs, target market analysis and import guide for wholesale buyers sourcing from Guangzhou Nansha Port.

Who Is ROX Motor and Why Importers Are Paying Attention

ROX Motor (极石汽车, pinyin: Jí Shí) is a Chinese new energy vehicle brand under BAW Group (Beijing Automobile Works), one of China’s oldest state-owned automotive conglomerates. Established in 2022 with a specific mandate to develop premium EREV SUVs for domestic and export markets, ROX Motor launched its first vehicle — the ROX 01 — in 2023, followed by the substantially upgraded ROX Adamas in December 2025.

The Adamas launch event was held in Abu Dhabi — a deliberate signal that ROX Motor’s commercial ambitions are weighted toward export from day one. The brand targets annual global sales of 30,000 units in 2026, with priority markets including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Southeast Asia, and South Africa (source: ROX Motor global sales press release, December 2025).

For international importers, ROX Motor sits at the intersection of two high-growth trends: the global appetite for large-format luxury SUVs and the accelerating shift toward extended-range electric vehicles (EREV) in markets where pure EV charging infrastructure is still developing. The Adamas is built to capitalise on both.

Globalucar Vehicle supplies ROX Adamas units FOB Guangzhou Nansha Port to buyers across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and South America. Both the 7-seat All-Terrain Edition and 6-seat Executive Edition are available for single-unit or bulk wholesale orders.

EREV Powertrain: 1,405 km Range Without Range Anxiety

The ROX Adamas is built on an Extended Range Electric Vehicle (EREV) architecture — sometimes called a range extender or series hybrid. This is distinct from a standard PHEV or pure BEV, and the distinction matters for importers in markets where charging infrastructure is limited or unreliable.

How it works: a 1.5T four-cylinder petrol engine acts solely as a generator to charge the battery — it never directly drives the wheels. All propulsion comes from the dual electric motors (front and rear). This architecture delivers EV smoothness at all speeds, with the refuelling convenience of a petrol vehicle.

Key powertrain specifications:

  • Battery: 44.5 kWh CATL ternary lithium — the same battery supplier used in BYD’s top-tier export vehicles
  • Combined system power: 350 kW / 740 N·m
  • 0–100 km/h: 5.5 seconds
  • Pure EV range (CLTC): 215 km — covers urban daily driving without fuel
  • Total combined range (CLTC): 1,405 km — no range anxiety even in markets with zero charging infrastructure
  • WLTC total range: 1,171 km (internationally calibrated figure)
  • DC fast charge (20–80%): approximately 27 minutes at 80 kW peak
  • V2L external discharge: 5.7 kW — powers camping equipment, site tools, or emergency loads

For context: the Li Auto L9 — the closest EREV competitor — offers 1,315 km total CLTC range. The Adamas exceeds it at 1,405 km. The BMW X5 45e PHEV offers only 87 km electric range and approximately 600 km total range, at roughly 70% above the Adamas’s export price point (EUR 89,000+ in Europe).

Interior and Cabin Technology

The Adamas interior is built around a triple-screen cockpit: a 15.7-inch centre touchscreen, 12.3-inch digital instrument cluster, and a 15.7-inch rear entertainment screen — all running on Qualcomm Snapdragon 8155, the same platform used in premium BYD and Li Auto models. The system supports over-the-air (OTA) updates, delivering software improvements post-delivery without workshop visits.

Sound is delivered through a 14-speaker 7.1 surround system — a specification typically found in European luxury SUVs priced above USD 80,000. A smart refrigerator (8.5L capacity) is standard in the centre console — a practical feature in hot-climate markets such as the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and sub-Saharan Africa.

The key interior difference between the two variants:

  • All-Terrain Edition (7-seat): Second row — heating, ventilation, lumbar massage, and leg rest. Comfortable for large families on long journeys.
  • Executive Edition (6-seat): Second row replaces the bench with individual aviation-style captain’s chairs featuring 8-point full-back shiatsu massage (neck, shoulder, back, lumbar), extended leg rest, heating, and ventilation — the configuration used in Chinese government fleet and premium ride-share deployments.

Off-Road Capability: Real Numbers, Not Marketing

The Adamas off-road credentials are measurable, not aspirational:

  • Battery ground clearance: 324 mm. Critical for markets with unmade roads — Nigeria, Angola, Tanzania, parts of Indonesia. The Land Rover Defender’s unladen air-suspension clearance is 291 mm. The Adamas exceeds this as a standard figure protecting a 44.5 kWh battery pack.
  • Wading depth: 770 mm. Tested in Extreme Escape terrain mode. The Toyota Land Cruiser 300 Series has a factory wading depth of 700 mm. The Adamas exceeds it.
  • Approach angle: 27.5° / Departure angle: 27.9°. Sufficient for steep incline approaches without bumper contact — relevant for mountainous markets such as Ethiopia, Ecuador, and the Philippines highlands.
  • Air suspension travel: 140 mm over 7 levels. High-ride mode maximises clearance for rough terrain; low mode eases entry and exit in urban settings.
  • 7 terrain modes including Sand and Mud. Electronic terrain management pre-sets torque distribution and stability thresholds for each surface type.

The only comparable EREV full-size SUVs by specification are the Li Auto L9, AITO M9 (Huawei/Seres), and Luxeed R7 — all priced above RMB 350,000 in China, none with the Adamas’s combination of wading depth and air suspension at this export price point.
