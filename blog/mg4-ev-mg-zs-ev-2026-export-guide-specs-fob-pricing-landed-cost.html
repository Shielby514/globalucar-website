2026 MG4 EV & MG ZS EV Export Guide: Europe-Ready Electric Hatchback & SUV from China

Why MG4 EV & MG ZS EV Lead Chinese EV Exports

The 2026 MG4 EV hatchback and MG ZS EV compact SUV are the two most important electric vehicles SAIC Motor exports under the MG brand. MG is the strongest-selling Chinese car brand in Europe — the MG4 EV was the best-selling Chinese EV in the UK in 2024 and 2025, and the MG ZS EV has been a top seller in Australia, Thailand, and across emerging markets. For export buyers, the pair covers the two most requested body styles in the EV market: a sporty rear-wheel-drive hatchback and a practical family SUV, both built on SAIC's dedicated electric-only MSP (Modular Scalable Platform).

This guide covers the export versions available from our Nansha, Guangzhou warehouse. Both vehicles ship with full CCC certification, UN38.3 battery compliance, and MIIT export documentation. FOB pricing starts at USD $17,800 for the MG4 EV 51kWh and $19,800 for the MG ZS EV 51kWh, with volume tiers at 5+ and 20+ units. We also keep the petrol MG5 sedan (from $7,200 FOB) in the same container plan for importers who want to mix electrified and conventional models in one shipment.

MG4 EV Full Specifications: Hatchback Platform

The MG4 EV is built on the MSP platform — a dedicated EV architecture with a rear-mounted motor, rear-wheel drive, and a thin floor-mounted LFP battery. This layout gives the MG4 EV a 50:50 weight distribution and the sharpest handling in its class, which is why it has won European car-of-the-year shortlists and strong fleet demand in the UK and Germany.

Charging, Battery & Real-World Range

Both MG models use LFP (lithium iron phosphate) chemistry — the same thermal-stability profile as BYD's Blade Battery, without the nickel-cobalt supply-chain cost. LFP cells tolerate higher operating temperatures (safe up to 60°C cell surface), which suits Middle East and African climates where summer cabin temperatures can exceed 55°C. Cycle life is 2,000+ full charges to 80% capacity, roughly 400,000-500,000 km of usable battery life.

DC fast charging: the MG4 EV charges 30-80% in about 26 minutes on a 117 kW charger; the ZS EV takes about 36 minutes on its 88 kW limit. Home charging on a 7 kW wallbox: the MG4 EV (51 kWh) takes about 7.5 hours for 0-100%; the ZS EV takes 7.3 hours. For importers in markets without public DC networks, every MG EV ships with the Type 2 CCS2 charging port (EU standard) — verify your market's charging standard before ordering, because the CCS2-combo inlet cannot be field-converted to CHAdeMO or GB/T.

Real-world range depends on driving style and climate. In warm-climate city driving (25-35 km/h average), the MG4 EV delivers 320-340 km per charge; highway driving at 120 km/h cuts this to 260-280 km. The ZS EV's larger pack and SUV aero profile return 390-410 km city / 310-330 km highway. Buyers planning daily routes over 300 km should choose the ZS EV or the higher-capacity 64 kWh MG4 EV variant (available on request).

Target Markets & Import Compliance Notes

United Kingdom: The MG4 EV's home export market. MG has UK dealer coverage in 140+ locations and the MG4 EV was the best-selling Chinese EV in the UK in 2024-2025. Import duty on Chinese-built EVs to the UK is currently 10% (MFN rate; no anti-dumping duty applies as of August 2026, but UK TTP reviews are ongoing — confirm the duty rate with your broker at booking time). VAT is 20%.

Australia: Zero import duty under the China-Australia FTA, 10% GST, and established MG dealer network. The ZS EV is the volume seller; the MG4 EV appeals to private buyers and fleets wanting a sport hatchback. The main cost is ocean freight from Nansha to Sydney or Melbourne ($1,200-$1,400/unit).

UAE and Saudi Arabia: 5% import duty plus 5% VAT (UAE) or 15% VAT (Saudi). Summer heat favors LFP chemistry. The UAE's EV registration incentives (free Salik tags, priority parking) push MG4 EV demand in Dubai, where the 351 km WLTP range is more than enough for daily commutes.

Kenya and Nigeria: Kenya applies 0% import duty on pure EVs (2024 waiver) — the cheapest African landing point at $21,918 landed. Nigeria has no EV duty waiver; standard 20% duty plus 7.5% VAT applies. In both markets, the lack of public fast charging makes the ZS EV's 440 km range and LFP battery the stronger proposition.

Thailand: BOI-registered EV imports enter at 0% duty. The Thai government's EV 3.5 scheme requires approved importers to assemble or source locally in later years — new importers should partner with an existing BOI licensee or accept the standard 60% duty for direct imports. MG has a factory in Thailand (SAIC-CP, Chonburi), so check whether import duty is avoidable by sourcing locally built units.

Brazil: Chinese EV imports face 18% import duty plus state ICMS (~18%). The federal government's 2026 EV incentive program provides a temporary PIS/COFINS exemption for EVs priced under R$250,000, which the MG4 EV qualifies for — verify the program is still active at import time. MG's Brazilian dealer network is expanding and offers strong residual values.

FAQ: 2026 MG4 EV & MG ZS EV Export from China

What is the FOB price of the MG4 EV and MG ZS EV?

The MG4 EV 51kWh Luxury is USD $17,800 FOB Nansha at single-unit pricing, dropping to $17,400 at 5+ units and $17,000 at 20+. The MG ZS EV 51kWh is $19,800 FOB, $19,400 at 5+, and $19,000 at 20+. Prices include CCC certificate, UN38.3 battery documentation, MIIT files, standard export packing, and pre-shipment inspection. A 64 kWh MG4 EV long-range variant is available on request at a $2,500 premium.

Are MG4 EV and MG ZS EV made in China or Europe?

Export units from our Nansha warehouse are built by SAIC Motor's Nanjing and Zhengzhou plants in China. MG also assembles some models at its Chonburi, Thailand plant (SAIC-CP) and has announced a European assembly operation for 2027, but current export volume to the UK, Australia, and Africa ships from Chinese plants. Buyers sourcing for Thailand should ask whether locally assembled units qualify for the BOI duty exemption.

What is the real-world driving range of the MG4 EV?

The MG4 EV 51kWh has a WLTP-certified range of 351 km. In warm city driving expect 320-340 km per charge; at 120 km/h highway speed expect 260-280 km. The figure is WLTP, not CLTC — Chinese-market CLTC ratings run 10-15% higher than real driving, so WLTP numbers are the conservative, reliable ones for export buyers.

How does the MG4 EV compare to the BYD Dolphin?

The MG4 EV is $5,092 more expensive than the BYD Dolphin 410km ($17,800 vs $12,708 FCA) but offers 79% more power (170 vs 95 hp), rear-wheel drive, a 2,705 mm wheelbase versus 2,700 mm, and WLTP range certification. The Dolphin wins on price; the MG4 wins on driving dynamics, power, and fleet resale appeal in Europe and Australia. Choose the Dolphin for price-first emerging markets, the MG4 EV for markets where the buyer values performance and RWD.

What warranty does the MG4 EV and MG ZS EV carry for export?

Both models carry a 7-year / 150,000 km vehicle warranty and a 7-year / 150,000 km battery warranty on LFP packs (whichever comes first), honored through MG's global dealer network in most markets. Export buyers should confirm warranty transferability with their destination country's MG distributor — in markets without an MG dealer, Globalucar provides a 12-month exporter warranty covering battery and drivetrain defects, with claims processed through our Guangzhou service center.

Can I mix MG EVs and petrol MG5 sedans in one container?

Yes. A 40ft HQ container fits 2 EVs + 2 MG5 sedans (mixed load), which spreads container cost across units. The MG5 1.5L Manual is $7,200 FOB (id=213), the MG5 1.5L Manual is $7,600 (id=208), and the MG5 1.8T Turbo Global Flagship is $8,700 (id=125). Mixed loads are a practical way for first-time importers to test both the EV and petrol markets in one shipment.
