How to Keep OE Suffixes and Multiple References Clear in a Parts RFQ

Published 12 November 2026 · Globalucar

Keep each requested part reference exactly as supplied, including its suffix and version segments. When a purchasing list contains two references on one line, retain both for review; do not assume that they identify interchangeable parts. This checklist explains how to prepare an unambiguous enquiry rather than assigning technical meaning to a particular suffix.

Should a trailing letter be removed?

Do not remove it simply because a search returns more results without it. Keep the original reference in one field and any search variation in a separate field. Ask the quotation team to identify the requested item before an alternative is accepted.

How should two references on one line be handled?

Keep the source line intact and label the reference relationship as unconfirmed. Ask whether they are alternatives, separate requested items or a historical reference. Do not duplicate a line quantity across both references without clarification.

What information makes an RFQ easier to review?

Provide the full reference, available description, quantity and unit, vehicle details, destination and photographs of labels or distinguishing features. State which information is missing so that the enquiry does not turn an assumption into an order specification.

Does an exact catalogue match prove compatibility?

No. A matching text reference is useful for locating a record. It does not by itself confirm the actual part, vehicle application, interchangeability, inventory or delivery date. Confirm the proposed item and the basis of identification in the quotation.

How should the final quotation be checked?

Compare every quoted line with the original requested reference. Review any alternative separately. Confirm quantities, units, product identification, packing and commercial terms before approving the order.
