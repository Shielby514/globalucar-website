Nansha Port, Guangzhou: Why It's Becoming the #1 Choice for Vehicle Exporters

📅 March 15, 2026 ⏱ 7 min read ✍️ Globalucar Editorial
Quick Answer: Discover why Nansha Port in Guangzhou is the preferred departure point for vehicle and auto parts exporters: RoRo terminals, bonded warehouses, weekly sailings to 60+ countries.

Nansha Port — Why Guangzhou's Deep-Water Auto Terminal Matters for Exporters

Nansha Port handled 26.4 million TEUs in 2025, ranking among the world's top five container ports and operating as South China's premier vehicle export port. For car export operations, Guangzhou Nansha Port offers three dedicated RoRo berths with capacity for 800,000+ vehicles per year, direct weekly sailings to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, East Africa, and West Africa, a bonded logistics zone with 24-hour customs clearance capability, and a direct rail connection to the Guangzhou South freight yard.

For Globalucar Vehicle, the decision to base operations at Nansha Port is straightforward: it's the closest deep-water port to the Pearl River Delta automotive manufacturing cluster. Factories supplying BYD, GAC, Chery, and Toyota are all within 60 km — reducing inland freight costs and shortening the total supply chain. Contact us to calculate your total landed cost from Nansha Port.

RoRo vs Container Shipping — Which Method for Your Vehicle Export

For most new car export and used car export operations, the choice between Roll-on/Roll-off (RoRo) and container shipping comes down to vehicle type, value, and destination market requirements:

MethodBest ForCost per Vehicle (SE Asia)Risk Level
RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off)Standard new and used vehicles, high-volume shipments$800-$1,500Low (purpose-built car carrier ships)
Container 20'HCModified, high-value, or LHD vehicles for RHD markets$1,400-$2,200Low (fully enclosed, lockable)
Container 40'HC2-3 standard sedans or SUVs per container$600-$900 per vehicleLow

RoRo is the default for standard Chinese cars export and used vehicles. Containers make economic sense for high-value vehicles, modified vehicles, or left-hand-drive vehicles destined for right-hand-drive markets where cosmetic protection during transit matters.

Nansha Port Sailing Schedule — Key Export Routes

Globalucar Vehicle ships vehicles and auto parts from Nansha Port weekly to key export destinations. Current approximate sailing schedules:

  • Guangzhou to Singapore / Malaysia / Indonesia: 3 departures/week, 7-10 day transit
  • Guangzhou to UAE (Jebel Ali): 2 departures/week, 18-22 day transit
  • Guangzhou to Kenya (Mombasa): 1-2 departures/week, 24-28 day transit
  • Guangzhou to Nigeria (Lagos): Weekly, 28-35 day transit
  • Guangzhou to Chile / Peru: Weekly, 35-42 day transit

Bonded Warehouse Benefits for Vehicle Exporters

Globalucar operates a bonded warehouse in Nansha Port's Free Trade Zone — the key advantage for buyers sourcing Chinese cars or auto parts wholesale:

  • Consolidation: Vehicles and parts from multiple factories can be consolidated under one roof before export, reducing individual shipments.
  • VAT deferral: No Chinese VAT is triggered on goods held in the bonded zone — only when leaving for domestic consumption in China.
  • Pre-export inspection (PDI): Pre-delivery inspection and photography can be conducted on-site before the vessel arrives at destination.
  • Pre-clearance: Export customs declaration can be pre-prepared 72 hours before vessel cutoff, eliminating last-minute delays.

Export Documentation & Customs Clearance from Nansha Port

Globalucar Vehicle handles complete car export documentation on behalf of buyers. Standard export package from Nansha Port:

  • Commercial Invoice & Packing List — at FOB value for customs purposes
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Sea Waybill — legal document of title issued by the shipping line
  • Certificate of Origin (FORM A or FORM E) — for preferential tariff under GSP and ASEAN-China FTA schemes
  • Vehicle Inspection Certificate — issued by China Customs (CIQ certificate)
  • VIN Declaration — full vehicle identification number list for the shipment

Why Globalucar Bases Its Export Operations at Nansha Port

The logic is simple: Nansha Port is the closest deep-water port to the Pearl River Delta automotive manufacturing cluster. BYD's main production facilities, GAC Toyota, and the Chery Guangdong plant are all within 60 km. Inland freight costs from these factories to Nansha Port are the lowest in South China, and port fees are competitive with both Yantian and Shekou.
