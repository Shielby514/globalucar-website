Geely Coolray, VW T-Roc, Skoda Tharu XR & Jetta VS5: Best-Value New SUVs for Export in 2026

📅 April 22, 2026 ⏱ 8 min read ✍️ Globalucar Editorial
Quick Answer: FOB Guangzhou from $8,100 — Geely Coolray, VW T-Roc, Skoda Tharu XR and Jetta VS5 are in stock now with factory-authorised direct export. MOQ 24 units, valid through June 30, 2026. Compare specs, prices and suitability for your market.

Why These Six Models Are the Export Sweet Spot Right Now

Finding new vehicles with factory-authorised direct supply at competitive FOB prices — with real stock on the ground and a firm validity window — is rare. In Q2 2026, Globalucar has secured exactly that for six models across two brand groups: Geely and the Volkswagen–Skoda–Jetta China joint venture portfolio.

When sourcing auto parts from China, understanding the difference between OEM and aftermarket options is critical for your business success.

Here is why this window matters for importers:

  • Factory-direct supply: These are new vehicles exported directly under manufacturer authorisation — not grey-market units or dealer surplus. You receive full factory documentation including Certificate of Conformity, China Inspection and Quarantine (CIQ) certificate, and MIIT/model approval sheet for each vehicle.
  • Confirmed inventory on hand: 100 units of each variant are available in bonded warehouse or can be factory-ordered against confirmed allocation. No waiting for production slots.
  • Price validity through June 30, 2026: FOB prices shown in this article are locked until end of June. After that, model-year pricing and exchange rates make these price points uncertain.
  • MOQ of 24 units: This is the minimum order quantity at which the listed prices apply. Below 24 units, per-unit pricing increases. Full 40-foot container loads of mixed models can also be arranged — ask about mixed-container pricing.

All six models ship FOB Guangzhou Nansha Port — China's second-largest port complex, with weekly RoRo and container sailings to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and South America.

Geely Coolray — Three Variants for Every Budget

The Geely Coolray (缤越) has been a consistent export success story since its 2019 debut. Built on Geely's modular BMA platform — the same architecture underpinning several Volvo-derived models — it delivers a level of engineering refinement that punches well above its price point in emerging markets.

In 2026, three variant tiers are available for export, giving importers the flexibility to target different customer segments within a single model line:

Coolray Basic Manual — US $8,100 FOB (id: product 121)

The entry-level Coolray pairs a 1.5TD turbocharged 3-cylinder engine (93 kW / 126 hp) with a slick 5-speed manual gearbox. At $8,100 FOB, it is competitively priced against equivalent-aged Japanese used compacts — but you are buying a brand-new vehicle with zero mileage, full warranty documentation, and factory finish.

  • Engine: 1477 cc DOHC turbo, 3-cylinder — 93 kW / 126 hp / 152 Nm
  • Transmission: 5-speed manual
  • Dimensions: 4380 × 1810 × 1615 mm | Wheelbase: 2600 mm
  • Ground clearance: 180 mm | Kerb weight: 1,310 kg
  • Features: 4 airbags, ABS+EBD, traction control, 45L fuel tank, 330L boot
  • Best for: price-sensitive markets (Africa sub-$12,000 retail segment, Central Asia, South America)

Coolray Battle (GK) — US $11,650 FOB

The Battle variant (缤越战神) is the performance-oriented mid-tier. It replaces the basic engine with the 1.5TD high-output unit (128 kW / 172 hp / 290 Nm) mated to a 7-speed wet dual-clutch (DCT) gearbox — delivering 0–100 km/h in 7.9 seconds, making it one of the quickest vehicles in its price class globally.

  • Engine: 1477 cc turbo — 128 kW / 172 hp / 290 Nm
  • Transmission: 7DCT wet dual-clutch
  • Suspension: MacPherson front + multi-link rear independent (upgraded vs. Basic)
  • Safety: 6 airbags, full ADAS suite (AEB, LKA, ACC, BSD)
  • Fuel tank: 59L | Boot: 400L
  • Best for: performance-oriented buyers in Southeast Asia and the Middle East; spec-focused fleet operators

Coolray Full Option with Package (GF+) — US $11,950 FOB

The top-tier Coolray adds a premium comfort and technology package to the Battle's mechanical specification. Same 128 kW engine and 7DCT gearbox, but with leather power seats, electric tailgate, rear-seat entertainment system, and Geely's CoolBox in-cabin cooler/heater.

  • All Battle mechanicals PLUS:
  • Leather power-adjustable driver seat
  • Electric tailgate with hands-free operation
  • Rear-seat entertainment display
  • CoolBox in-cabin temperature-controlled storage
  • Best for: dealerships targeting family buyers; markets where interior specification closes sales (Gulf retail, Southeast Asia premium compact segment)
Importer tip — Coolray in LHD markets: All three Coolray variants are LHD (left-hand drive). They are optimally positioned for Southeast Asia (Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Indonesia), Middle East (GCC), Africa (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya's LHD exception segment), and South America. Do not import Coolray into RHD markets such as Australia, UK, or East Africa's RHD-only countries (Kenya standard, Tanzania).

VW T-Roc & Skoda Tharu XR — European DNA, China Price

The Volkswagen T-Roc and Skoda Tharu XR are among the most compelling value propositions in this portfolio. Both are products of the SAIC-Volkswagen joint venture — manufactured in China to global Volkswagen Group quality and engineering standards, but priced for China's hyper-competitive domestic market. The result: European-badge quality at a price impossible to achieve through direct European factory supply.

Volkswagen T-Roc Full Option Black — US $19,500 FOB

The T-Roc is Volkswagen's entry-level compact SUV, positioned globally between the Golf and the Tiguan. The China-market "Black" edition adds a distinctive blacked-out trim package — panoramic sunroof, 19-inch black alloy wheels, sports body kit, and piano-black interior inserts — making it a stand-out specification at this price point.

  • Engine: 1.4 TSI (150 hp) or 2.0 TSI (220 hp depending on trim) — VW Group EA211/EA888
  • Transmission: 7-speed DSG dual-clutch
  • Dimensions: 4234 × 1819 × 1573 mm | Wheelbase: 2590 mm
  • Key features: Panoramic glass roof, adaptive suspension, Digital Cockpit Pro, travel assist, IQ.DRIVE suite
  • Safety: 7 airbags including knee airbag, AEB, LKA, ACC
  • Best for: markets where VW badge carries premium positioning — Southeast Asia (Thailand, Malaysia), Middle East (UAE, Saudi), North Africa (Algeria, Morocco)

The T-Roc Black at $19,500 FOB competes favourably against Grey-import T-Rocs from Europe that arrive at $22,000–$25,000 for equivalent specification, while offering the added benefit of full factory-origin documentation from a recognised Chinese JV manufacturer.

Skoda Tharu XR Full Option — US $14,500 FOB

The Skoda Tharu is the Volkswagen Group's Skoda-badge compact SUV for the Chinese market — effectively a sister model to the T-Roc on the same MQB platform. The XR variant adds an "off-road aesthetic" package: raised ride height (185 mm ground clearance), skid plates, XR badging, and an Adventure Green interior accent package.

  • Engine: 1.4 TSI — 110 kW / 150 hp, paired with 7-speed DSG
  • Platform: VW MQB (shared with Golf, Tiguan, T-Roc) — proven reliability in 50+ markets
  • Dimensions: 4467 × 1841 × 1628 mm | Wheelbase: 2648 mm — notably longer than the T-Roc
  • Key features: Virtual cockpit, Skoda Simply Clever storage solutions, panoramic sunroof, 360° parking camera
  • Best for: Skoda markets in Central Europe, Eastern Europe, North Africa, parts of Southeast Asia; buyers wanting VW Group mechanics at Skoda pricing

At $14,500 FOB versus the T-Roc's $19,500, the Tharu XR offers a $5,000 per-unit cost advantage on the same MQB platform. For fleet buyers or distributors in cost-sensitive markets, this gap translates directly into retail margin or competitive pricing power.

Jetta VS5 — The Compact SUV Built for Emerging Markets

The Jetta brand is Volkswagen's value sub-brand for China — positioned below the mainline Volkswagen marque in price, but sitting on the same proven platform architecture (MQB A0). The VS5 Full Option is a 5-door compact SUV that has found strong traction in export markets precisely because it delivers recognisable VW Group quality at a price point accessible to a much wider buyer base.

  • Price: US $13,400 FOB Guangzhou
  • Engine: 1.4 TSI — 110 kW / 150 hp @ 5,000–6,000 rpm
  • Transmission: 7-speed DSG (Aisin 6AT option available)
  • Dimensions: 4418 × 1841 × 1616 mm | Wheelbase: 2630 mm
  • Boot: 482L — among the largest in class
  • Key features: 360° panoramic camera, 12-inch touchscreen, ADAS safety suite, 18-inch alloy wheels

The VS5 is particularly well-matched to markets where VW brand recognition drives buyer confidence but sticker price is a critical barrier. It sits $1,100 below the Tharu XR and $6,100 below the T-Roc, making the three models a natural good/better/best ladder within the VW Group offering.

VW Group export note: T-Roc, Tharu XR and Jetta VS5 are all LHD vehicles produced by the SAIC-VW and FAW-VW joint ventures in China. They carry full VW Group type approval documentation and are compliant with Euro 6 emissions standards. Appropriate for LHD markets; confirm homologation requirements with your local standards authority before ordering.

How to Place a Factory-Direct Order Before June 30

The June 30, 2026 price validity deadline is firm — it reflects the factory allocation window and the exchange rate band used to calculate these FOB figures. Here is how to move efficiently from enquiry to confirmed order:

  1. Select your model(s) and confirm quantity: The minimum order quantity for the prices above is 24 units per variant. For mixed shipments (e.g. 12 Coolray Basic + 12 Jetta VS5), contact us directly — mixed-model pricing is available but requires individual quotation.
  2. Request a formal pro-forma invoice (PI): Send your order details to ucars@global-ucars.com. Include: model, variant, quantity, destination port, and your preferred incoterm (FOB or CIF). We issue a PI within 24 hours on business days.
  3. Arrange deposit payment: Standard payment terms are 30% deposit against PI, 70% balance against copy of Bill of Lading. T/T (telegraphic transfer) is the standard payment method. L/C (Letter of Credit) can be arranged for orders of 50+ units.
  4. Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI): All vehicles undergo a full PDI at our Nansha bonded facility before loading — OBD scan, exterior walk-around photography (100+ images per vehicle), fluid check, and tyre pressure verification. PDI report is included in the documentation package.
  5. Documentation package: Each shipment comes with Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Vehicle Export Certificate, CIQ Inspection Certificate, MIIT production certificate, and factory PDI report. Additional documents (e.g. COC for your destination market, Arabic-language invoice) can be prepared upon request.
  6. Transit time: RoRo vessel from Nansha Port — indicative transit times: Southeast Asia (Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia) 7–12 days; Middle East (Jebel Ali) 16–20 days; West Africa (Lagos) 25–30 days; East Africa (Mombasa) 22–26 days.
New vehicles at these FOB prices — with factory authorisation and complete documentation — represent one of the strongest value propositions currently available from any Chinese exporter. The $8,100 Geely Coolray Basic, in particular, positions a brand-new SUV below the cost of comparable three-year-old used Japanese imports in many markets. The window closes June 30, 2026.
