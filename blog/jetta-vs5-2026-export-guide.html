JETTA VS5 2026: What Importers Need to Know

The JETTA VS5 is a compact SUV built by FAW-Volkswagen's JETTA sub-brand in Chengdu, China, sharing the MQB platform with the VW Tiguan and Skoda Karoq. For importers in Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia, the VS5 offers German engineering at a Chinese price point — the 2026 DSG Full Option model ships at $13,100 FOB Nansha, roughly 40 percent below a comparable VW Tiguan built in Germany. With a 1.4T EA211 engine producing 110 kW and 250 Nm, the VS5 delivers adequate power for urban commuting and light highway duty in markets where fuel economy matters more than outright acceleration.

Globalucar Group exports the JETTA VS5 from Nansha Port, Guangzhou, to 50+ countries. This guide covers real FOB pricing, technical specifications, container loading calculations, and country-by-country landed cost analysis to help importers make informed purchasing decisions. The VS5 has proven especially popular in Nigeria, Saudi Arabia, and the Philippines, where its VW lineage commands trust and its price undercuts Japanese and Korean competitors by $3,000-$5,000.

The JETTA VS5 uses Volkswagen's EA211 1.4-liter turbocharged engine paired with a 6-speed DSG dual-clutch transmission. The MQB platform provides a proven chassis shared across multiple VW Group vehicles, which means spare parts availability is excellent in markets where VW already has a dealer network. JETTA VS5 vs Competitors in Target Markets

In the compact SUV segment, the JETTA VS5 competes against the Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, and Toyota Corolla Cross. The VS5's advantage is its VW MQB platform — the same architecture used in the VW Tiguan — at a price point 35-45 percent lower. The EA211 1.4T engine, while less powerful than the Tucson's 2.0L naturally aspirated unit (110 kW vs 115 kW), delivers 250 Nm of torque from 1,750 rpm, giving the VS5 stronger low-end response for city driving and loaded acceleration.

For importers in markets with existing VW dealer networks (South Africa, UAE, Philippines), the VS5 benefits from shared service parts availability. Oil filters, brake pads, and suspension components for the MQB platform are interchangeable across VW, Skoda, and JETTA models, reducing the parts inventory burden for multi-brand dealers.

However, importers should note that JETTA is positioned as a budget brand within the VW Group, meaning interior materials and sound insulation are a step below the VW Tiguan. The VS5 uses hard plastic door panels and cloth seats in the base trim, while the Full Option adds synthetic leather and a power driver's seat. For fleet buyers and value-conscious consumers in Africa and the Middle East, this trade-off is generally acceptable given the $8,000-$12,000 price gap to a Tiguan.

Globalucar provides full export documentation for the JETTA VS5, including:

  • CCC Certificate — China Compulsory Certification for the vehicle
  • Manufacturer's Certificate of Origin — issued by FAW-Volkswagen Chengdu plant
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List — for customs declaration
  • SASO Certificate — for Saudi Arabian shipments (SABER platform registration)
  • SONCAP Certificate — for Nigerian shipments (Standards Organisation of Nigeria conformity assessment)
  • Egypt GoEIC Registration — for Egyptian shipments

For markets requiring left-hand-drive vehicles only, the JETTA VS5 is produced exclusively in LHD configuration. Importers in RHD markets (UK, Australia, parts of East Africa) should consider alternative models. The VS5 meets Euro 5 emission standards, which is accepted in most target markets though some countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia) are transitioning to Euro 6 requirements by 2027.

What is the FOB price of JETTA VS5 2026 from China?

Is the JETTA VS5 a real Volkswagen?

The JETTA VS5 is built by FAW-Volkswagen in Chengdu, China, on the VW MQB platform shared with the VW Tiguan and Skoda Karoq. It uses the EA211 1.4T engine and DSG transmission from the VW Group parts bin. While JETTA is positioned as a budget sub-brand, the core mechanicals are genuine VW Group components.

How many JETTA VS5 units fit in a shipping container?

The JETTA VS5 (4,419mm length) fits 2 units per 40ft high cube container with interior loading, or 3 units per 40ft flat rack container. For larger orders, ro-ro shipping from Nansha Port accommodates 8-12 vehicles per shipment at lower per-unit freight cost.

What is the landed cost of JETTA VS5 in Nigeria?

The estimated landed cost of the JETTA VS5 DSG Full Option in Lagos, Nigeria is approximately $19,162, including FOB price ($13,100), sea freight ($1,750), 20 percent import duty ($2,970), and 7.5 percent VAT ($1,342). This leaves room for a competitive retail price against the Hyundai Tucson and Toyota Corolla Cross.

Does the JETTA VS5 support 92 RON petrol?

Yes. The EA211 1.4T engine is designed for 92 RON regular petrol, which is widely available and affordable in African and Middle Eastern markets. This is a practical advantage over turbocharged competitors that require 95 RON premium fuel.

Are spare parts available for JETTA VS5 in Africa and the Middle East?

The JETTA VS5 shares its MQB platform with VW Tiguan, Skoda Karoq, and Audi A3, meaning engine, transmission, suspension, and brake parts are interchangeable. In markets with existing VW dealer networks (South Africa, UAE, Philippines), service parts are readily available.
