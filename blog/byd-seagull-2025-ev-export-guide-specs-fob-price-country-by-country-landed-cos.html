      PAA FAQ: BYD Seagull Export Answers

      d="faq">PAA FAQ: BYD Seagull Export Answers

      Q: What is the BYD Seagull FOB export price from China?

      The BYD Seagull FOB Nansha export price is $8,500 for the base Vitality 305 km trim, $9,200 for the mid-range Free 305 km, and $10,500 for the top-spec Flying 405 km with ADAS. These are promotional export prices through Globalucar as of July 2026. Bulk orders of 10+ units receive 3-5% per-unit discount. Compare this to the Wuling Air EV at $7,500 (3-door, 300 km) — the Seagull's 5-door configuration and stronger motor justify the $1,700 premium.

      Q: What is the real-world range of the BYD Seagull?

      The 30.08 kWh version delivers 230-260 km real-world range in mixed city-highway driving (vs 305 km CLTC). The 38.88 kWh version achieves 310-350 km (vs 405 km CLTC). Range decreases by 15-20% with AC running continuously at 35°C+ ambient temperature (relevant for Middle East and African markets). In cold climates (below 5°C), range decreases by 25-30% due to battery heating and cabin warming. The BYD Blade Battery (LFP chemistry) handles high ambient temperatures better than NMC alternatives — a critical advantage for tropical markets.

      Q: Does the BYD Seagull come in right-hand drive?

      Currently, the BYD Seagull is only produced in left-hand drive (LHD). This limits direct sales in RHD markets like Kenya, Tanzania, South Africa, UK, Australia, and Japan. However, BYD has confirmed RHD production is under development, with Thailand and South Africa identified as priority RHD markets. Expected RHD availability: Q4 2026 - Q1 2027. For importers in RHD markets considering early entry, some countries (e.g., Kenya and Tanzania) allow LHD vehicle registration for commercial/fleet use. Check your local vehicle registration regulations before ordering.

      Q: How much does it cost to ship a BYD Seagull by sea?

      RO-RO (roll-on/roll-off) shipping rates for the BYD Seagull (compact vehicle, ~1,200 kg) from Nansha Port: Manila ~$320/unit, Bangkok ~$380/unit, Dubai ~$650/unit, Mombasa ~$980/unit, Santos (Brazil) ~$1,150/unit, Manzanillo (Mexico) ~$680/unit, Lagos ~$1,250/unit. Rates are for 4+ unit consolidated shipments. Container shipping (2-3 units per 40-ft container) adds $150-250 per unit but provides better protection for high-value trims. All rates are July 2026 estimates and fluctuate with fuel surcharges and seasonal demand.

      Q: Are there any import restrictions or special requirements for EV imports?

      Requirements vary by country: Brazil — INMETRO certification required (Globalucar assists with documentation), 0% import duty for EVs under quota; Philippines — EO 12 provides 0% duty through 2028, no special EV certification needed; Kenya — KEBS inspection certificate required, 10% EV import duty; Nigeria — SONCAP certificate required, 10% duty on new vehicles; UAE — ESMA compliance certificate required, GCC standard charging port (GB/T to CCS2 adapter available). Globalucar provides a complete export documentation package. Importers are responsible for destination-country compliance — we recommend working with a local customs broker familiar with EV imports.

      Q: What is the HS code for electric vehicle export from China?

      Pure electric passenger vehicles (BEV): HS 8703.80. This classification applies to all battery electric vehicles regardless of battery capacity or motor power. Key distinction: HS 8703.80 is for pure EVs only. Plug-in hybrids (PHEV) fall under HS 8703.60 (rechargeable with external power source) or HS 8703.40 (hybrid). Most countries offering EV incentives (duty reduction or exemption) specifically reference HS 8703.80 in their customs tariff schedules. Always verify the exact subheading with your destination customs broker.
