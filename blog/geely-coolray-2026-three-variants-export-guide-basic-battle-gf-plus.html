2026 Geely Coolray: Three Variants from $8,100 FOB — A Complete Export Buyer’s Guide

📅 May 17, 2026 ⏱ 10 min read ✍️ Globalucar Editorial
Quick Answer: The 2026 Geely Coolray is available in three export variants: Basic Manual ($8,100), Battle ($11,650) and Full Option GF+ ($11,950) — all on the BMA platform with 217 units in stock. Complete specs comparison, MOQ 24, price valid through June 30, 2026, FOB Guangzhou Nansha Port.

Why the Geely Coolray Is the Best-Value New SUV for Export in 2026

At US $8,100 FOB Guangzhou, the 2026 Geely Coolray Basic Manual is the lowest-priced brand-new SUV available for factory-direct export from China — period. It is not a used vehicle, not a grey-market unit, not a reconditioned import. It is a zero-mileage, factory-fresh compact SUV with full documentation, warranty coverage and manufacturer authorisation.

To put that $8,100 figure in context: it is below the landed cost of a three-year-old Toyota Corolla in many African markets, below the CIF price of a five-year-old Honda HR-V in Southeast Asia, and roughly equivalent to the wholesale price of a mid-mileage Nissan Qashqai in the Middle East. The Coolray Basic gives importers the opportunity to offer a brand-new vehicle with full warranty at the price point of a used car.

Globalucar currently holds 217 units of Geely Coolray across all three variants in bonded warehouse stock, with factory-direct allocation confirmed. FOB prices are valid through June 30, 2026.

Geely and the BMA Platform: Engineering Credibility

Geely Automobile Holdings is China’s largest privately-owned automotive group, with a portfolio that includes Volvo Cars, Polestar, Lotus, Proton and the Geely, Zeekr and Geometry brands. The Coolray is built on Geely’s BMA (Basic Modular Architecture) platform — the same architecture that underpins several Volvo-derived models and has been in production since 2018.

Why the BMA Platform Matters

  • Volvo-derived engineering: The BMA platform was developed with input from Geely’s Volvo Cars subsidiary, incorporating Volvo’s safety engineering principles, crash structure design and material specifications.
  • High-strength steel body: 72% high-strength and ultra-high-strength steel in the Coolray’s body structure, with hot-stamped steel in the A-pillar, B-pillar and roof rail for rollover protection.
  • Proven in global markets: BMA-platform vehicles (Coolray, Binrui, Emgrand) have been exported to over 30 countries since 2019, with established homologation in Middle Eastern, Southeast Asian, Central Asian and Latin American markets.
  • Scalable architecture: BMA supports multiple wheelbase lengths and powertrain configurations, allowing Geely to offer a range of variants from a single platform — which translates to consistent parts availability and simplified service training for importers.

Three Variants, Three Buyer Segments

What makes the Coolray export opportunity particularly attractive is the three-tier variant structure. Importers can target different market segments within a single model line — simplifying logistics, documentation and dealer training while maximising addressable market coverage.

Coolray Basic Manual — $8,100 FOB

The entry-level Coolray pairs a 1.5TD turbocharged 3-cylinder engine (93 kW / 126 hp) with a 5-speed manual gearbox. It is the ultimate price-point play for markets where sticker price is the primary purchase driver.

  • Engine: 1,477 cc DOHC turbo, 3-cylinder — 93 kW / 126 hp / 152 Nm
  • Transmission: 5-speed manual
  • Dimensions: 4,380 × 1,810 × 1,615 mm | Wheelbase: 2,600 mm
  • Ground clearance: 180 mm
  • Kerb weight: 1,310 kg
  • Features: 4 airbags, ABS+EBD, traction control, 45 L fuel tank, 330 L boot, manual air conditioning, basic infotainment with Bluetooth
  • Product ID: 121

Who Should Import the Basic Manual?

  • African budget markets: Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya (LHD exception segment), Tanzania — where sub-$12,000 retail positioning captures maximum buyer volume
  • Central Asian markets: Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan — where manual transmission is preferred and import duty is calculated on CIF value
  • Government fleet and institutional buyers: Procurement tenders that specify new vehicles within a strict budget ceiling
  • Ride-hailing fleet operators: Uber, Bolt, InDrive operators seeking low-cost new vehicles for driver lease programmes

Coolray Battle (GK) — $11,650 FOB

The Battle variant (缤越战神) is the performance-oriented mid-tier. It replaces the basic engine with the 1.5TD high-output unit (128 kW / 172 hp / 290 Nm) mated to a 7-speed wet dual-clutch gearbox — delivering 0–100 km/h in 7.9 seconds.

  • Engine: 1,477 cc turbo — 128 kW / 172 hp / 290 Nm
  • Transmission: 7DCT wet dual-clutch
  • Suspension: MacPherson front + multi-link rear independent (upgraded from Basic’s torsion beam)
  • Safety: 6 airbags, full ADAS suite (AEB, LKA, ACC, BSD)
  • Fuel tank: 59 L | Boot: 400 L
  • Product ID: 203

Who Should Import the Battle?

  • Southeast Asian premium compact segment: Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia — where performance spec and ADAS features justify a higher retail price
  • Middle East spec-focused buyers: GCC markets where buyers compare specifications closely and multi-link rear suspension is a selling point
  • Young buyer demographic: Markets with a growing young professional class that values performance and technology over badge prestige

Coolray Full Option GF+ — $11,950 FOB

The top-tier Coolray adds a premium comfort and technology package to the Battle’s mechanical specification. Same 128 kW engine and 7DCT gearbox, but with leather power seats, electric tailgate, rear-seat entertainment and Geely’s CoolBox in-cabin cooler/heater.

  • All Battle mechanicals, plus:
  • Leather power-adjustable driver seat
  • Electric tailgate with hands-free operation
  • Rear-seat entertainment display
  • CoolBox in-cabin temperature-controlled storage compartment
  • Product ID: 204

Who Should Import the GF+?

  • Gulf retail showrooms: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman — where interior specification and premium features close sales in competitive dealer environments
  • Family-oriented markets: Southeast Asia premium compact segment where rear-seat entertainment and electric tailgate are strong selling points
  • Dealerships upgrading from Japanese brands: Importers transitioning from used Japanese imports to new Chinese vehicles who need a premium variant to anchor their showroom range

Best Markets and Buyer Segments for Each Variant

Importer tip — Coolray in LHD markets: All three Coolray variants are LHD (left-hand drive). They are optimally positioned for Southeast Asia (Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Indonesia), Middle East (GCC), Africa (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya’s LHD exception segment) and South America. Do not import Coolray into RHD markets such as Australia, UK, or East Africa’s RHD-only countries.
  • Basic Manual ($8,100): Sub-Saharan Africa, Central Asia, South America budget segments — markets where price is the primary purchase driver and manual transmission is accepted or preferred.
  • Battle ($11,650): Southeast Asia performance segment, Middle East spec-focused buyers, young professional demographic — markets where powertrain performance and ADAS features are valued.
  • GF+ ($11,950): Gulf retail showrooms, premium compact segments, family buyers — markets where interior comfort and technology features close showroom sales.

Export Process, Documentation and How to Order

  1. Select variant(s) and quantity: Minimum 24 units per variant at listed prices. Mixed shipments (e.g. 12 Basic + 12 Battle) available — contact for individual quotation.
  2. Request pro-forma invoice: Email ucars@global-ucars.com with variant, quantity, destination port and incoterm preference (FOB or CIF). PI issued within 24 business hours.
  3. Deposit payment: 30% deposit against PI, 70% balance against copy B/L. T/T standard; L/C for 50+ unit orders.
  4. PDI and loading: Full PDI at Nansha bonded facility — OBD scan, 100+ exterior photographs, fluid check, tyre pressure verification. PDI report included.
  5. Documentation: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, B/L, Certificate of Origin, Export Certificate, CIQ certificate, MIIT production certificate, factory PDI report. CoC for destination market available on request.
  6. Transit times: RoRo from Nansha: Southeast Asia 7–12 days, Middle East 16–20 days, West Africa 25–30 days, East Africa 22–26 days.
