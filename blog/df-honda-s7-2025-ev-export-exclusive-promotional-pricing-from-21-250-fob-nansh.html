Procurement & Shipping Process

  1. Inquiry: Contact Mr Didi at Globalucar with your preferred trim, quantity, and destination port.
  2. Proforma Invoice: We issue a detailed PI with FOB pricing, vehicle specifications, and estimated shipping schedule.
  3. Deposit: 30% T/T deposit to confirm the order. Balance payable before shipment or against B/L copy (for established buyers).
  4. Pre-Shipment Inspection: Optional third-party inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas) at buyer's cost. Standard documentation included at no extra charge.
  5. Loading: Container loading or RO-RO vessel at Nansha Port, Guangzhou. RO-RO recommended for 1-5 units; container for mixed loads.
  6. Shipping Documents: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (GCC FTA or China-Asean FTA), Vehicle Inspection Certificate, Export License.
  7. Destination Clearance: Importer handles customs clearance. Globalucar provides all China-side documentation.

Shipping time estimates: Gulf ports (Jebel Ali, Dammam): 18-22 days RO-RO. West Africa (Apapa Lagos, Tema): 28-35 days. East Africa (Mombasa, Dar es Salaam): 22-28 days. Central Asia (Almaty via rail): 20-25 days. Latin America (Callao, Buenaventura): 35-42 days.

PAA FAQ: Honda S7 Export Answers

Q: Is the DF Honda S7 a genuine Honda product?

Yes. The DF Honda S7 is manufactured by Dongfeng Honda Automobile Co., Ltd., a 50:50 joint venture between Honda Motor Co. (Japan) and Dongfeng Motor Corporation (China). It carries full Honda engineering, Honda quality control standards, and Honda powertrain technology. All vehicles come with factory warranty documentation and complete export certification. This is not a rebadged domestic car — it is a Honda-developed EV built in Honda's dedicated EV production line.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity for the promotional price?

The promotional FOB pricing ($21,250/$22,550) applies to single-unit orders. For bulk orders of 5+ units, contact us for additional fleet discount pricing. A 20-unit full container order qualifies for the deepest discount tier. Mixed orders (e.g., 3x Explorer+High Speed + 2x Explorer+Comfort) are accepted. This is an exclusive Globalucar promotional offer — stock is limited and available on a first-come, first-served basis.

Q: Does the Honda S7 support right-hand drive?

Currently, the DF Honda S7 is available in left-hand drive (LHD) only. It is suitable for all LHD markets including the Middle East, Africa (except RHD countries), Central Asia, Latin America, and Eastern Europe. For RHD markets (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, South Africa, Pakistan, Bangladesh), we can source alternative Honda models — contact us with your RHD requirements.

Q: What charging standard does the S7 use?

The DF Honda S7 uses the CCS2 (Combined Charging System Type 2) standard for DC fast charging, which is the dominant standard in Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and most global markets outside China and Japan. For AC charging, it uses the Type 2 (Mennekes) connector. Home charging with a 7 kW wall box provides a full charge in approximately 8-9 hours.

Q: What warranty and after-sales support is included?

Standard warranty: 3 years or 100,000 km (whichever comes first) for the vehicle, 8 years or 160,000 km for the high-voltage battery. Globalucar provides all factory warranty documentation. For parts support, our Kingmay Auto Parts division can supply Honda OEM-grade replacement parts. We recommend importers stock basic consumables (brake pads, cabin filters, wiper blades) with the initial vehicle order.

Q: How does the S7 compare to the BYD Atto 3 for export?

The S7 is a mid-size SUV (4,750mm) vs the Atto 3's compact size (4,455mm) — offering more interior space and road presence. The Honda brand carries stronger recognition and trust in markets like the Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia compared to BYD. Price-wise, the S7 Explorer+High Speed ($21,250) slots between the Atto 3 Comfort ($19,500) and Atto 3 Premium ($22,800). For customers who value brand trust and Honda reliability, the S7 offers a compelling package at a promotional introductory price.
