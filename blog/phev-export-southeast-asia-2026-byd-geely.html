Market-by-Market Overview: Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam for Chinese PHEV Exports

🇹🇭 Thailand — Most Mature PHEV Import Market in ASEAN

Thailand is the most mature NEV market in ASEAN for 新能源汽车出口, with a dedicated government target of 30% EV/PHEV sales by 2030. Chinese brands — particularly 比亚迪出口东南亚 — have established local assembly partnerships under Thailand's EV 3.0 incentive scheme. Importers who qualify as "approved brands" benefit from reduced import duty (0–2% under the ASEAN FTA) and government subsidy pass-through. 比亚迪 PHEV lineup already holds Thai TISI type approval for key models. For 插电式混合动力汽车出口 to Thailand, the EV 3.0 subsidy of up to THB 150,000 per unit significantly improves landed cost competitiveness.

🇲🇾 Malaysia — Geely & Proton Drive PHEV Adoption

Malaysia's National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR) provides full import duty exemption and excise duty exemption for approved CBU EVs and PHEVs through end-2025, with extensions under review. 吉利新能源汽车出口 brands — specifically PROTON with Geely's technology — have grown significantly through local assembly and dealer networks that benefit parallel 广州新能源汽车出口 importers. CKD assembly investment is driving brand recognition across all income segments, creating strong pull-through for 中国新能源汽车出口 to Malaysian distributors.

🇮🇩 Indonesia — High Tariff, High Opportunity for Fleet Buyers

Indonesia remains a high-tariff market for CBU vehicles (40–50% import duty on most categories), but the government's Low Carbon Emission Vehicle (LCEV) program offers duty relief for vehicles meeting energy efficiency thresholds — making it a selective but profitable market for 新能源汽车出口. 比亚迪出口东南亚 opened its first Indonesian factory in West Java in Q1 2026, improving local availability — though export demand for smaller fleets and niche models continues through direct 广州新能源汽车出口 import channels.

🇻🇳 Vietnam — ACFTA Drives 0% Duty for Chinese PHEVs

Vietnam removed the special consumption tax (SCT) surcharge on domestically assembled EVs in 2023 and extended the policy to eligible imported models in 2025. PHEVs from 中国新能源汽车出口 are benefiting from preferential ASEAN-China FTA tariffs (ACFTA Form E), dropping effective import duty from 70% to 0% for qualifying vehicles — a game-changing advantage for 插电式混合动力汽车出口. VinFast's local dominance is being challenged by 比亚迪 and Geely on price and features, particularly in the $20,000–$30,000 SUV segment where 吉利 Galaxy L7 is gaining market share rapidly.

Shipping & Documentation for PHEVs from Guangzhou Nansha Port

  • RoRo shipping (滚装船运输): Accepted by all major operators without special battery pre-discharge requirements. State of Charge (SoC) of 30–50% still recommended as best practice for Nansha Port departures.
  • Classification (海运分类): IMDG Class 9 (miscellaneous dangerous goods) applies to the onboard traction battery, but most shipping lines treat PHEV shipments the same as ICE vehicles in practice — no special handling surcharges.
  • Documentation required (出口单证): Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, ACFTA Form E (Certificate of Origin), Vehicle Inspection Certificate (CIQ), Battery Safety Data Sheet (from manufacturer), and destination port's import permit where required.
