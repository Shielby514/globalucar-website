Price Breakdown: US $6,800/unit (FOB Nansha)

At US $6,800/unit (FOB Nansha), this vehicle includes port handling, container loading, and customs clearance at Nansha Port. This is a FOB (Free on Board) price, meaning:

  • Included: Vehicle cost, port handling fee, container loading, export customs declaration, export inspection certificate, bill of lading
  • Not included: Ocean freight, marine insurance, destination port charges, import duties, destination customs clearance, local delivery

For a typical 40-foot container shipment to Lagos, Nigeria, total CIF cost would be approximately $8,300 per unit including sea freight (~$1,200 for West Africa) and insurance (~$300). Import duties vary by country — Nigeria charges 35% on new vehicles, while Kenya applies 25% CET plus 20% VAT.

Primary target markets:

  • West Africa (Nigeria, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire): High demand for affordable new vehicles. Roewe (SAIC) brand recognition is growing rapidly through Chinese infrastructure projects. Taxi and ride-hailing operators represent the largest buyer segment.
  • East Africa (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda): Right-hand drive availability makes this a natural fit. Kenya's BRT system expansion creates demand for fleet vehicles.
  • Middle East (Iraq, Jordan, Oman): Hot climate durability, strong demand for SUV configurations. GCC import regulations are straightforward for Chinese vehicles.
  • Southeast Asia (Philippines, Indonesia, Cambodia): Price-sensitive markets where Japanese vehicles are 40-60% more expensive. Roewe (SAIC) offers comparable quality at half the price.
  • Latin America (Peru, Ecuador, Colombia): Growing middle class, limited local production. Chinese vehicle imports grew 85% in 2025 across the Andean region.
