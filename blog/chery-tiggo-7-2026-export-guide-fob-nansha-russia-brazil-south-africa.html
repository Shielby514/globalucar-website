Chery Tiggo 7 2026 Export Guide: FOB Nansha Prices, Specs & Market Fit for Russia, Brazil & South Africa

📅 2026-10-06 ⏱ 13 min read ✍️ Globalucar Editorial
Quick Answer: The 2026 Chery Tiggo 7 is China's best-selling export SUV nameplate: a 4.55 m five-seat crossover with 1.5T (108 kW) and 1.6T TGDI (145 kW / 290 N·m) engines plus a Super Hybrid PHEV, FOB Nansha from $11,900. This guide covers the full specification table, the FOB price ladder with container loading, Russia EAC certification, Brazil tariff routing via Caoa Chery, South Africa's 10-year engine warranty market, and landed-cost snapshots for five markets.

Ask any vehicle export desk in Guangzhou which Chinese model moves the most units per square metre of ship deck, and the answer is rarely a flagship. It is the compact SUV — the segment where Chinese engineering meets the price point that Russia, Latin America, Africa and the Middle East actually buy at volume. Within that segment, the Chery Tiggo 7 is the reference product: Chery's longest-running export nameplate, now in its second-generation facelift, and the default comparison point against the Haval Jolion, Changan CS35 Plus and the used-Japanese competition that dominates every emerging market below $20,000.

This guide sets out the 2026 Tiggo 7 export range as we quote it from Nansha: the two petrol powertrains and the Super Hybrid PHEV, indicative FOB pricing by trim, the certification route for Russia and the EAEU, the tariff arithmetic that decides whether Brazil orders ship from China or from Caoa Chery's local assembly, and the warranty structure that has made South Africa Chery's fastest-growing export market. All prices are FOB Nansha for LHD units; RHD is available on order for select trims.

Why the Tiggo 7 Is Chery's Export Volume Engine

Chery has been China's largest passenger-vehicle exporter for over two decades, and the Tiggo 7 sits at the centre of that trade for three reasons. First, it hits the price-performance intersection: a 2,670 mm wheelbase five-seater with a 1.6T direct-injection turbo engine at a price where rivals offer naturally-aspirated 1.5-litres. Second, it is engineered for bad fuel and bad roads from the start — 92 RON tolerance, 196 mm ground clearance and a suspension tune that survives the broken asphalt of Lagos, Almaty and the Russian regions. Third, it has the parts depth: five years of steady export volume means filters, brake components, suspension bushings and body panels are stocked by independent wholesalers across every market the model has entered.

For a distributor, that third point matters more than any spec-sheet line. A model's retail price is set once at launch; its service network decides whether the dealer can sell it for a decade. The Tiggo 7 crossed that threshold in Russia, Kazakhstan, South Africa, Chile and the Gulf years ago, which is why first-time importers keep landing on it as their opening order: the market already knows the car, and the parts channel already exists. Our earlier guides cover the larger siblings — the Tiggo 8 Pro / Tiggo 9 for Russia and Central Asia — and the equivalent Haval products (H6 and H9 export guide).

Tiggo 7 2026: Engines, Trims and Equipment

The 2026 export range runs three powertrains. The 1.5T is the fleet and price-entry unit — 108 kW and 210 N·m through a 7-speed wet dual-clutch, tuned for 92 RON fuel and cheap servicing. The 1.6T TGDI is the volume retail engine: 145 kW and 290 N·m, 0-100 km/h in 8.3 seconds, with a multi-link rear suspension and 19-inch wheels on upper trims. The Super Hybrid PHEV pairs a 1.5-litre hybrid engine with dual electric motors and an 18.4 kWh CATL lithium-iron-phosphate battery — 150 kW system output, roughly 90 km of electric range and a combined range beyond 1,100 km, which positions it for markets where fuel cost, not infrastructure, is the constraint.

Equipment is where the Tiggo 7 wins its comparison tests: dual 12.3-inch screens, wireless CarPlay and Android Auto, a 540-degree camera, heated and ventilated front seats on upper trims, and a full Level 2 ADAS suite (AEB, adaptive cruise, lane centring, blind-spot monitoring) that arrives as standard on the 1.6T Prestige and above. The model carries a five-star Euro NCAP rating with six to eight airbags depending on trim. Russia-spec units add an engine block heater and cold-climate battery calibration; Gulf-spec units carry upgraded air conditioning for 50°C ambient operation. Russia and the EAEU: Certification and the Parallel-Import Reality

Russia remains the Tiggo 7's largest single export market, and the model has been a fixture in the country's top-ten SUV charts since 2023. Imports clear under the EAC framework (TR CU 018/2011) with SBKTS per-unit approval for commercial lots; the practical cost items are the utilization fee — raised again for 2025-2026 — plus recycling-deposit mechanics and the GLONASS-era ERA-GLONASS emergency system requirement, which is now handled at the port-of-entry fitting stage. Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Armenia share the same EAC framework, and the Khorgos rail route (18-22 days from Guangzhou) has made Almaty the stocking hub for Central Asian distribution. Uzbekistan's staged import-duty reductions since 2023 have opened a market where the Tiggo 7's 92 RON tolerance and parts availability through Chery's Tashkent dealer network give it a service advantage over less-established Chinese entrants.

Brazil and Latin America: The Tariff Decision

Brazil is the market where sourcing strategy matters most for the Tiggo 7. Fully-built imports from China face a 35% import duty plus IPI and ICMS stacking, which prices direct container shipments out of retail contention. Caoa Chery, Chery's Brazilian partner, assembles the Tiggo 7 family locally at its Jaguarina plant in São Paulo state — so Brazilian orders are quoted through the local-assembly channel, while the China trade serves Uruguay, Paraguay and Bolivia from port stock. Chile is the opposite case: zero duty under the China-Chile FTA makes it the most price-transparent Tiggo 7 market in Latin America and the reference point for regional price benchmarking. Mexico applies zero duty to Chinese-built vehicles under its own framework but taxes by value; Peruvian and Ecuadorian distributors order steadily at container tier. For a Latin American entrant, the practical question is not whether to stock the Tiggo 7 but which trim — the 1.6T Prestige carries the equipment list that wins comparison shopping in Santiago and Lima, while the 1.5T fleet trim serves ride-hailing and corporate orders.

South Africa and Africa: Warranty Depth as a Sales Weapon

South Africa has become the Tiggo 7's showcase market because Chery South Africa markets a 5-year/150,000 km vehicle warranty backed by a 10-year/1,000,000 km engine warranty for first owners — the longest powertrain cover in the segment, and the single strongest counter to the used-Japanese default. Nigerian, Ghanaian and Kenyan importers order the 1.5T for urban retail where the 92 RON tolerance and simple turbo hardware keep field repairs cheap; the 1.6T serves buyers stepping up from a first car. Two spec decisions matter on African orders: confirm the full-size spare (standard on Prestige trims, optional on fleet) and specify underbody coating for coastal markets. Parts support runs through the same Kingmay-serviced channel as other Chinese brands — a front-end parts set for the Tiggo 7 costs roughly 40% of the equivalent Toyota RAV4 package, which is the number fleet operators in Lagos and Nairobi actually calculate on.

Landed Cost Snapshots for Five Markets

Landed cost decides which trim wins each order. The two most-quoted builds are set out below with indicative duty, freight and tax components — planning estimates, not quotations.

Tiggo 7 1.5T (FOB $11,900) — fleet build:

  • Chile (San Antonio): 0% duty under China-Chile FTA + RORO freight ~$2,300 + 19% VAT on CIF → landed ≈ $16,900
  • Russia (Vladivostok): EAEU duty 15% + VAT 20% + utilization fee + RORO freight ~$1,400 → landed ≈ $19,800
  • South Africa (Durban): 18% duty + ad valorem + 15% VAT + freight ~$1,900 → landed ≈ $19,300

Tiggo 7 1.6T Prestige (FOB $14,300) — retail build:

  • UAE (Jebel Ali): 5% duty $715 + freight ~$1,700 + 5% VAT on CIF → landed ≈ $17,700
  • Kazakhstan (rail via Khorgos): EAEU duty 15% + rail freight ~$2,400 + VAT → landed ≈ $21,200

Two notes. Chile's zero duty is why its retail price is the regional benchmark — the landed figure is almost pure FOB plus logistics, making Santiago the cleanest comparison market for trim pricing. Russia's utilization fee now adds more than the duty itself on sub-2.0-litre petrol units, which has pushed the Russian order book toward higher trims where the fixed fee amortizes better against equipment value.

Ordering Process, Lead Time and Mixed Loading

A first Tiggo 7 order through our desk follows six steps: trim and market-spec confirmation against your country's homologation rules, proforma invoice with FOB or CIF terms, 30% deposit, production slot booking (3-4 weeks if the trim is not in port stock), pre-shipment inspection with photo report, and balance against shipping documents. In-stock units at Nansha ship within 10 days of deposit. Mixed loading keeps first orders low-risk: a typical trial container pairs two or three Tiggo 7 units with a parts package — filters, brake pad sets, suspension bushings, LED lighting — sized to six months of service demand, so the dealer opens with both sales stock and workshop stock in one clearance. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 2026 Chery Tiggo 7 export price from China?

The range runs $11,900 for the 1.5T fleet trim, $12,600 for the 1.5T Prime, $14,300 for the 1.6T Prestige, $15,400 for the 1.6T AWD and $18,900 for the Super Hybrid PHEV — all FOB Nansha at container tier. Volume discounts of 2-4% apply above ten units, and four units fit a single 40-foot high-cube container.

Is the Tiggo 7 available with all-wheel drive?

Yes, on the 1.6T powertrain for select export markets at $15,400 FOB — roughly $1,100 over the equivalent FWD trim. Most volume markets order FWD, since the 196 mm ground clearance and 92 RON tolerance cover the majority of use cases; AWD matters mainly in Central Asia's winter regions and Andean Latin America.

Can the Tiggo 7 be imported into Russia in 2026?

Yes. Clearance runs under the EAC framework (TR CU 018/2011) with SBKTS per-unit approval for commercial lots, plus the utilization fee and ERA-GLONASS fitting. The Tiggo 7 has been among Russia's best-selling Chinese SUVs since 2023, so parts supply and dealer familiarity are established — the practical planning item is the utilization fee, which now exceeds the customs duty on sub-2.0-litre petrol units.

How does the Tiggo 7 compare with the Haval Jolion for import?

The Jolion offers a 30 mm longer wheelbase and a marginally lower FOB at equivalent trim; the Tiggo 7 counters with 35 kW more power and 70 N·m more torque from its 1.6T, standard Level 2 ADAS on the Prestige, and deeper independent parts availability across more export markets. Where GWM's dealer network is denser (Russia, South Africa), the Jolion retails faster; elsewhere the Tiggo 7's power-per-dollar wins the comparison tests.

What is the Super Hybrid PHEV's electric range?

Roughly 90 km of pure-electric range from an 18.4 kWh CATL lithium-iron-phosphate battery, with a combined range beyond 1,100 km once the 1.5-litre hybrid engine engages. It charges from a domestic socket overnight, so it suits markets where fuel cost rather than charging infrastructure is the constraint — and where duty regimes treat PHEVs favourably against pure petrol.

What warranty does the Tiggo 7 carry on export markets?

Five years or 150,000 km on the vehicle for most export markets. In South Africa, Chery backs the engine with a 10-year/1,000,000 km first-owner warranty — the segment's longest powertrain cover and the strongest retail argument against used-Japanese competition. Fleet buyers elsewhere should negotiate warranty terms into the supply agreement, as regional structures vary.
