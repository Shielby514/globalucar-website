Radiator Cap RFQ Checklist: Which Details Should a Buyer Send?

Published 22 November 2026 · Globalucar

Send the complete reference, a readable photograph of its markings, the stated pressure and units, the vehicle identification available to you, and the required quantity. If the reference is uncertain, describe it as an identification request. A cap that looks similar, or carries the same pressure marking, should not be treated as a confirmed replacement without a catalogue or supplier verification.

What information helps identify the cap?

Keep the number exactly as supplied, including leading zeros and suffixes. Record the marking on the part separately from a supplier catalogue number: they may serve different purposes. Add the vehicle make, model, model year and engine information where known. A VIN can support a catalogue check, but a VIN alone does not establish that a particular photograph shows the correct component.

Photograph the top markings, underside and sealing area clearly. Include the packaging label if available and keep each photo tied to the same enquiry row. If no readable number is present, say so rather than substituting a similar-looking search result.

Is the pressure marking enough?

No. Gates manufacturer listings publish pressure rating alongside filler-neck dimensions and coolant-recovery-system information. Those separate fields are a useful reminder that one value is not a complete specification. These catalogue examples do not establish an interchangeable reference for your vehicle.

Record the pressure exactly as marked, including its unit. Ask the quoting supplier to confirm the proposed item against the applicable catalogue. Do not use a higher or lower rating merely because the item is available. This checklist supports purchasing identification, not installation or pressure-setting advice.

How should a wholesale enquiry be organised?

Use one line per complete reference, with columns for quantity, marking, photo filename and unresolved questions. If two numbers appear on one source sheet, retain them separately until their relationship has been established. Do not combine order quantities on the assumption that the numbers are equivalent.

For example, a useful enquiry note is: “Please confirm this complete reference and the photographed markings, and identify any missing information before quoting.” Avoid labelling the request “confirmed fitment” when only a visual similarity has been checked.

What should the quotation confirm?

Ask for the proposed reference, manufacturer or brand as offered, catalogue basis for its application, a matching item photograph where available, quantity, unit, price currency and commercial terms. Stock, packing, lead time and shipping arrangements need order-specific confirmation. A reference page is not a stock reservation or a technical certification.

Can I request identification without a photograph?

Yes. Send the full reference and available vehicle details, and make the missing photograph explicit. Identification may require additional information. A missing image should remain a missing-image status until a corresponding photograph is checked; a generic cap image would not close that gap.
