When Is Canton Fair 2026? Full Phase Schedule for Auto Parts Buyers

📅 2026-10-08 ⏱ 11 min read ✍️ Globalucar Editorial

Every October the same five questions land in our in-box from auto parts distributors, importers and brand buyers across Africa, the Gulf, the CIS and Latin America. The first one — "when is Canton Fair 2026?" — is the easiest to answer. The second, third, fourth and fifth ("which phase covers auto parts?", "do I need a buyer badge?", "how long do I need in Guangzhou?", "is it worth combining with a factory visit?") are the ones that decide whether you actually place orders, or just walk the halls for three days and fly home with a tote bag.

This guide is a single, dated reference for the full Canton Fair 2026 schedule — Phase 1, 2 and 3 dates, opening hours, the auto parts halls, the three-day move-in gaps, and how an auto parts buyer should plan a seven-day trip around the calendar. Everything below is cross-checked against the official China Import and Export Fair site (cantonfair.org.cn) and the venue operator's published hall map. Dates are correct as of 8 October 2026. Re-confirm closer to your travel date, as the organizer occasionally adjusts single-day maintenance windows.

01 What "When Is Canton Fair 2026?" Really Means for an Auto Parts Buyer

The headline answer is short. Canton Fair 2026 runs from 15 October to 4 November 2026, broken into three five-day phases with three-day move-out / move-in intervals in between. For the 140th session the organizer has renamed the phases thematically — "Advanced Manufacturing" (Phase 1), "Quality Home Life" (Phase 2) and "Better Life" (Phase 3) — but the underlying hall layout is the same as in previous years.

For an auto parts buyer, the follow-up question is even more important: which of those three weeks contains your category? The answer is firmly Phase 1, 15–19 October 2026. Vehicles, two-wheelers, auto components, auto electronics, motorcycle parts, NEV charging hardware, and the wider "advanced manufacturing" cluster all sit inside Phase 1. Phases 2 and 3 carry home, gift, fashion and lifestyle categories — useful for some buyers who mix product lines, but irrelevant if your business is purely automotive aftermarket or OEM-grade parts.

The other common misreading is to assume the fair is one continuous event. It is not. Each phase has its own exhibitor list, its own hall map, and its own peak visitor days. Most overseas auto parts buyers fly in for Phase 1 only, then either return home or move on to a factory visit in the Pearl River Delta during the 20–22 October break before Phase 1 ends. We will get to that cadence in section 6.

04 Why Phase 1 Is the Only Phase Most Auto Parts Buyers Need

Phase 1 of the 140th Canton Fair is, for the global auto parts industry, the most important single week of the year. It is where the bulk of brake, filter, suspension, electrical, engine, transmission and rubber-moulded part suppliers are physically present under one roof. If you only attend one phase, it is Phase 1.

Three operational reasons reinforce this. First, supplier concentration: by the organizer's published hall plan, the vehicle and component halls sit in Areas A and B of the Pazhou complex, with overflow in the International Pavilion for cross-border sellers. Second, decision-maker presence: factory owners, export managers and tooling engineers typically attend only Phase 1, because that is the week their customers fly in. Third, lead-time alignment: most export orders placed in mid-October can be tooled, sampled, and shipped before the Chinese New Year factory shutdown in late January / early February, which matters for distributors building inventory for Q1 and Q2 of the following year.

Phases 2 and 3 do still see a small number of crossover auto accessories exhibitors (dash cams, interior trim, organizers, lighting upgrades), but the engine of the auto parts business is unambiguously Phase 1.

For 2026, the organizer has grouped auto parts into the "Advanced Manufacturing" cluster, which physically sits in Areas A and B. 06 Three Phases, Three-Plus-Week Window: How the Intervals Affect Your Trip

The biggest planning mistake first-time Canton Fair visitors make is assuming the three phases run back-to-back. They do not. Between Phase 1 closing on 19 October and Phase 2 opening on 23 October, there is a full three-day gap, and the same again between Phase 2 and Phase 3. Those gaps are not optional rest days — they are hard move-out / move-in windows when the halls are physically closed for booth teardown, reconfiguration, and fresh exhibitor load-in.

For an auto parts buyer, those gaps are actually useful. If you are sourcing from Guangzhou or anywhere in the Pearl River Delta — Foshan, Dongguan, Huizhou, Zhongshan, Shenzhen — you can use the 20–22 October break to visit two or three factories in person, then return to Pazhou for a final round of follow-up meetings at the end of Phase 1 (some exhibitors stay late on 19 October for dinners and private meetings). Buyers from further afield often use the gap to fly to Shenzhen for cross-province factory visits, or to consolidate samples in a Guangzhou bonded warehouse.

What you cannot do is walk halls during the gap days. If your travel budget only allows one week, build the calendar around Phase 1 plus a same-province factory day, not around a hypothetical three-week grand tour.

07 The Daily Clock: Opening Hours, Last Entry and Travel Time

The official daily hours for the 140th Canton Fair are 09:30 to 18:00, all three phases, with the final entry cut-off typically around 16:30 to allow visitors time to reach the outer halls before close. Last exit is generally 17:30, with exhibitor-only teardown beginning at 18:00 sharp. Weekend days (17–18 October, 24–25 October, 31 October – 1 November) are full trading days — there is no "weekend only for locals" rule.

In practical terms, an experienced buyer's day at the fair looks like this:

Plan to be at the gate no later than 09:15 if you want a productive first hour.

08 How the 2026 Calendar Compares to Spring 2026 and Autumn 2025

The 140th Canton Fair follows the same spring/autumn cadence that has run since 1957. The Spring 2026 session (the 139th) ran 15 April – 5 May 2026 on the identical three-phase, five-day, three-gap structure. The Autumn 2025 session (the 138th) ran 15 October – 4 November 2025 on the same skeleton. The 140th is, by design, a continuation — same venue, same hours, same exhibitor categories, with only the new "Advanced Manufacturing / Quality Home Life / Better Life" branding changes.

For an auto parts buyer this consistency is operationally useful. Pricing trends you observed in spring 2026 (filter media, brake friction, rubber compounds) will, broadly, hold into autumn 2026 with seasonal adjustments. Container freight rates, raw steel and copper indices, and the RMB/USD corridor are usually the bigger swing factors than the fair's own calendar. The other continuity is online: the Canton Fair online platform runs year-round, so any supplier you met in April 2026 is still reachable in October 2026 through the same buyer login.

The 140th session's specific improvements, per the organizer's own announcements, are better international buyer fast-track lanes at the entrance gates, expanded on-site translation services, and a refreshed International Pavilion layout to accommodate more cross-border e-commerce exhibitors.

09 Coordinating Your Trip: Flights, Hotels and the Guangzhou Transport Grid

Flights into Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN) are heaviest on the Monday and Friday before each phase opens, which is why smart buyers arrive on Wednesday or Thursday for Phase 1 (15 October) and depart on the Tuesday or Wednesday after (20–21 October). Hotel rates in the Pazhou / Zhujiang New Town corridor typically triple in the 48 hours before Phase 1 opens, so lock rooms 6–8 weeks in advance.

Within the city, the metro is the only sensible way to reach Pazhou at opening time. Line 8 runs directly to Xingang Dong (Area A) and Pazhou (Area B) stations. Allow at least 30 minutes from a Tianhe hotel. Taxis are usable after 18:00 once the metro thins out, but the Pazhou bridge approach can add 20–30 minutes during the morning rush.

For buyers who also want a same-province factory visit during the 20–22 October gap, we typically arrange a half-day Guangzhou warehouse inspection and a one-night stay in Foshan or Dongguan, then a return to Guangzhou for outbound flight on 22 or 23 October. The whole arc — Phase 1 plus a Pearl River Delta factory detour — fits cleanly inside an eight-day window.

10 Frequently Asked Questions

When is Canton Fair 2026 — is it the same week every year?

Yes, the autumn session reliably runs 15 October to 4 November, broken into three five-day phases. The 140th session in 2026 follows the same skeleton, with Phase 1 on 15–19 October, Phase 2 on 23–27 October, and Phase 3 on 31 October – 4 November. Spring sessions run 15 April to 5 May on the identical structure.

Which phase of Canton Fair 2026 covers auto parts?

Phase 1 (15–19 October 2026). The "Advanced Manufacturing" cluster in Areas A and B of the Pazhou complex houses vehicles, two-wheelers, auto parts, auto electronics, NEV components and related hardware. Phases 2 and 3 do not feature auto parts as a primary category.

Do I need a visa to attend Canton Fair 2026?

Overseas buyers typically need a Chinese business visa (M visa) or a tourist visa (L visa) depending on the issuing country's agreement with China. The Canton Fair organizer can issue an official invitation letter to registered overseas buyers, which materially helps the visa appointment. Apply at least 6–8 weeks before travel; some consulates are faster, some slower.

How long should I plan to stay in Guangzhou for the auto parts week?

Most first-time auto parts buyers stay 5–7 days. A typical plan is: arrive Tuesday or Wednesday, attend the full Phase 1 week (15–19 Oct), use the 20–22 October gap for a same-province factory visit, and depart Friday or Saturday. Buyers who also want a deeper factory audit often extend to 10–12 days.

Can I attend Canton Fair without a buyer badge?

No. All overseas visitors must hold a Canton Fair buyer badge, obtained through the official pre-registration portal. The badge is free but takes 3–7 working days to process after identity verification. On-site registration is technically possible but the queues on Phase 1 opening day are long enough to cost you a productive morning.

Is the Canton Fair still worth attending in the age of online sourcing?

Yes, for the auto parts category specifically. The physical fair concentrates 5,000+ component exhibitors in one venue for five trading days, with decision-makers present and sample stocks on hand. Online platforms are excellent for follow-up and small re-orders, but they cannot replicate a 20-minute booth-side negotiation with the factory owner standing next to you. The two channels are complementary, not competing.
