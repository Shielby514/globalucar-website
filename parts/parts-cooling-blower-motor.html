Product Details

HVAC blower motors for in-cabin heating and ventilation systems. Each motor is matched to the OEM impeller fan cage dimensions and runs on PWM-controlled voltage from the blower resistor or HVAC control module. High-efficiency armature windings minimise current draw at lower fan speeds to reduce battery load. Motor commutators are copper-segmented and the brushes are carbon-composite for minimal arcing and long brush life. Case materials and mounting dimensions are matched to the original unit. Motors are supplied with or without the attached fan cage depending on buyer preference. Suited for Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Ford, GM, Hyundai, Kia, VW applications. MOQ 10 pcs.
