Product Details

Piezoelectric knock sensor with flat-response element for broadband detonation detection (5–18 kHz). Allows ECU to retard ignition on detected knock events, protecting engine from piston damage while maintaining efficiency. Resolves P0325–P0332. Stainless steel body, 200°C-rated harness, OEM mounting torque boss. Single-wire and two-wire designs available.
