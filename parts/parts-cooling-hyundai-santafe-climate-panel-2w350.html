Product Details

OEM-grade Hyundai Santa Fe DM (ix45) A/C Heater Climate Control Panel, OE reference 97250-2W350. Variant of the 97250-2W360 panel for 2013-2016 Hyundai Santa Fe DM models in the Middle East market. Independently tooled and manufactured by Kingmay Auto Parts — an exclusive product line developed specifically to address OEM supply shortages in the China market. Full validation testing completed; quality stable and production-ready. The panel provides complete cabin climate management: automatic dual-zone temperature control, A/C compressor switching, heater blend control, 5-speed blower fan regulation, air distribution mode selection (face / bi-level / feet / defrost / feet-defrost), and driver/passenger seat heating control. The CleanAir ionization function and AUTO/OFF rotary controls with LED-backlit display are identical to the factory original. Each unit is subjected to a rigorous quality assurance protocol: tact switch contact resistance measurement, rotary encoder detent step verification, LCD backlight uniformity check, infrared temperature sensor calibration reference, and full CAN-bus communication handshake with ECU emulator. Factory harness connector pinout — no wiring modification, no splicing, no re-pinning. Plug-and-play replacement that any workshop technician can install in under 20 minutes. Designed and endurance-tested for Middle East climate extremes. Anti-static ESD-protective packaging. Private labeling available for volume buyers (MOQ 50 pcs for custom packaging with your brand).
