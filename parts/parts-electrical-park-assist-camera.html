Product Details

OEM-style reverse/parking assist cameras providing a wide-angle rear view image to the vehicle's multimedia display when reverse gear is engaged. Our cameras use a 1/3" CMOS image sensor with a minimum 120° field of view and integrated parking guide lines (dynamic or static, switchable). IP68-rated housing withstands high-pressure car wash and extended exposure to rain, mud and road salt. Night vision performance is provided by built-in high-sensitivity low-light optics (minimum 0.1 lux). Video output options include NTSC/PAL composite, AHD and CVBS for compatibility with aftermarket and factory screen upgrade installations. Mount styles: flush-mount licence plate trim, top-mount bracket, embedded in handle recess. Reversing trigger wire connection. MOQ 10 pcs.
