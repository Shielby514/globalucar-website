Who We Are

Car & Auto Parts One-Stop Solution Provider

Guangzhou Vehicle External Trade Co., Ltd. operates under the Globalucar brand in Guangzhou, China. We support vehicle sourcing, multi-brand parts procurement and export order coordination for importers, dealers, distributors and fleet buyers.

Our service starts with your purchasing requirements: vehicle model and specification, or part references and quantities, together with the destination. We coordinate product enquiries, quotations, packing requirements and shipment arrangements for the proposed order.

Product specification, availability and commercial responsibilities are confirmed in the quotation and order documents.
